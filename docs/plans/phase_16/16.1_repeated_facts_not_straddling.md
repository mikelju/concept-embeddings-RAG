# Deviation 16.1: the 29 "straddling" facts are repeated sentences, not sentences crossing a break

> Status: **open, waiting for the author's decision.** Found 2026-09-30 at step S2, stop point 2.
> Nothing beyond `data/phase16/source/` has been written; no code was changed to make a count
> match.

## Context

Step S2 (`cer p16-data`) checks every count of the 2026-09-30 measurement before writing the
corpus and the questions. Every count reproduced except one: the spec's D2 says 29 facts
straddle a paragraph break; the stage found 0. It refused and stopped, as the plan says.

## Problem

The pre-spec note read "6,055 facts lie inside exactly one newline-delimited paragraph and 29
cross a paragraph break". Re-measured on the pinned files on 2026-09-30 (read-only, by the S2
agent and again by hand):

| Check | Count |
|---|---:|
| facts containing a newline | 0 |
| facts found verbatim in more than one newline paragraph of their article | 29 |
| facts occurring more than once in the article body | 29 |
| of those, cases where the two paragraphs are identical text | 0 |
| answerable queries affected | 29 of 2,255 (1.29 %) |

The 29 facts are sentences that the article prints twice, in two **different** paragraphs: a
lead bullet and the body sentence, a summary line and the section it summarizes (examples:
"Public Betting Patterns: If a significant amount of money…" and "If a significant amount of
money…"; a fantasy-football preview where a bullet is repeated inside a longer line). The
original diagnostic counted "paragraphs containing the fact" and read 2 as "crosses a break".

## Root cause

A mislabelled diagnostic in `16.pre_spec_notes.md`, carried into the spec's D2 ("a fact that
straddles a paragraph break maps to the unit holding its first sentence"). The rule is correct
for the case it names, but the case does not occur. The case that does occur, a fact printed in
two distinct paragraphs, has no rule in the spec. The plan's `body.find` (first occurrence) would
silently decide it, which is not a spec rule.

## Options for the author

**A. First occurrence (recommended).** The gold unit is the paragraph holding the fact's first
occurrence in the article body. One fixed rule, one gold unit per fact, the existing gold-set
contract unchanged. The other paragraph is a verbatim-containing near-duplicate; if a system
retrieves it instead of the gold one, the question counts as a miss. Bounded: at most 29
queries (1.29 %). To keep the possible false misses visible, the outcome records, deciding
nothing, how many of the 29 queries had the **other** paragraph inside the 2,048-token context
while the gold one was not, per system.

**B. Either paragraph satisfies the fact.** Gold becomes a set of alternatives for those 29
facts. Honest, but Full Support, gold recall and every stored contract of Phases 9-15 take one
gold set per question; the harness would need an "any of" gold, a change behind a measured
metric, for 1.29 % of the queries.

**C. Both paragraphs are gold.** Requires a system to retrieve a duplicated sentence twice;
inflates misses for no reason. Not recommended.

## Solution adopted

_Pending._ On the author's decision, D2 in `16.spec.md` is corrected in its own commit (the
measured facts replace the straddling sentence; the chosen rule is written), the constants in
`config.py` change from "29 straddling" to "29 repeated, 0 straddling", the loader lists the 29
repeated facts (qid, evidence position, article, both paragraphs' unit ids) with a digest, and S2
reruns.

## Impact

- `docs/plans/phase_16/16.spec.md` D2 (a frozen rule, corrected by this deviation).
- `docs/plans/phase_16/16.pre_spec_notes.md`: the sentence is corrected with a dated note; the
  measurement itself stays as the record of what was read.
- `src/concept_embeddings_rag/config.py`, `corpus/multihop_rag.py`, their tests, and the plan's
  C2 and technical notes.
- Option A adds one reported count to `outcome.json` (S6); options B and C change the harness.

## Propagation

After the decision: spec D2 first, then `16.0_multihop_rag_validation.md` (C2, gold-mapping
notes, S2 tick), then the pre-spec note. The master plan's Phase 16 paragraph does not change:
the scope is the same.
