## Objective

> With every weight and parameter frozen at its HotpotQA value, do the Entity Hop systems
> (P10-C and P14) still retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than Dense + BM25 on a
> corpus of news articles cut into the authors' own paragraphs?

One pass, nothing fitted, no LLM: P10-A, P10-B, P10-C and P14 at their frozen values (spec
table) on the 2,255 answerable MultiHop-RAG queries, over every newline paragraph of the 609
articles. Primary endpoint (D5): Full Support @2,048, **P14 against P10-B**, one two-sided exact
McNemar test, α = 0.05; labels `TRANSFER_SUPPORTED`, `TRANSFER_REGRESSION`,
`TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED`, `DATA_STOP`, written by code. Secondaries (D6) and every report
(ladder, D7-D11) decide nothing.

## Technical notes

### Article metadata: in the corpus line, not a sidecar

`fullwiki.load_corpus` reads only `unit_id`, `title` and `sentences` from each line and re-derives
the id from title and text; other keys are ignored. Each Phase 16 line therefore adds
`"article": {"index", "url", "source", "category", "published_at"}` (index = position in the
pinned `corpus.json`). Chosen over a sidecar because the spec's data contract names
`corpus.jsonl.gz` as holding "the units with their article metadata", one file goes to the pod,
and every existing reader (BM25, GLiNER through `indexable_text`, the token counter) sees the
units unchanged. The metadata is not covered by the unit id, so `corpus.json` records
`article_metadata_digest` over `(unit_id, index, url, source, category, published_at)` in unit
order and `multihop_rag.load_articles` recomputes it. A unit belongs to exactly one article
(titles are distinct and the title is inside the id).

### Gold mapping (D2)

- Each query gets `qid = f"mhr-{position:04d}"`, its position in the pinned `MultiHopRAG.json`
  (the file has no id; the SHA-256 pin fixes the order). `answer` is not copied (`Question.answer
  = ""`): it is never read.
- An evidence piece names its article by `title` (checked against `url` when present). The fact
  is located with `body.find(fact)` (first occurrence; facts found more than once are counted).
  - **Inside**: no `"\n"` in the matched span; gold = the unit of the paragraph holding it.
  - **Repeated** (deviation 16.1, option A): the fact occurs more than once in the body (29
    facts, 29 queries, each time in two distinct paragraphs). Gold = the unit of the
    paragraph holding the **first occurrence** (`body.find`). Each is listed as
    `repeated_facts` (qid, evidence position, article index, gold unit id and position, the
    later paragraphs' unit ids and positions) with a digest; the later paragraphs are not
    gold. `facts_inside` counts the other facts (6,055).
  - **Straddling**: the span contains `"\n"`. None does on the pinned source (deviation
    16.1); S2 checks the count is 0. Were one to occur, gold = the unit of the paragraph
    holding the match's first non-whitespace character, and it is listed with a digest.
  - **Unmapped** (article or fact not found): `fullwiki`'s sentinel via `phase9._sentinel(qid,
    position)`, kept in the denominator. More than 1 % of the 6,084 facts (the spec counts
    facts, not queries as Phase 15 did) sets `terminal_state = DATA_STOP`.
- Gold ids deduplicated per query in fact order (`dict.fromkeys`); `supporting_facts` holds
  `(article title, paragraph position)` per fact. Queries are grouped for D7 by gold count after
  deduplication, the counts the spec gives.

### D8: the same-article tally

For a system S in {P10-C, P14} against P10-B, per query: contexts come from
`phase15.recorded_contexts` (the stored fused lists packed at 2,048 tokens, checked against the
recorded Full Support). A gold unit **entered the context through the hop** if it is in S's
context, not in P10-B's, and listed in S's stored hop component (depth 100). Gold units that enter
without the hop listing them (the reweighting of Dense and BM25) are counted apart and not
attributed. **P1** is `record["hop"]["p1"]` (checked by `reach` to be Dense's first unit); its
article comes from `load_articles`. Each hop-entered gold unit is tallied as P1's article or
another article; given for won queries (S full, P10-B not) and all queries, overall and per
`question_type`. For context: the share of queries whose gold lies in one article (S2) and the
share whose P1 article holds at least one gold unit (from P10-A's stored Dense lists).

### D9: Hits@k over units

`Hits@k = Σ_q |gold(q) ∩ fused(q)[:k]| / Σ_q |gold(q)|` over the 2,255 queries, micro-averaged as
the spec words it ("fraction of gold units, over all queries"), sentinels in the denominator,
from the stored fused lists (P10-A's is the Dense list). Recorded beside the paper's with three
named differences: whole paragraphs, not 256-token chunks; another corpus cut; per-unit
micro-average, not the paper's evaluation script.

### GLiNER's window

A unit's `sentences` is `(text,)`, so `local_extraction.windows_for` reads an over-long unit as the
title then the paragraph, and the model truncates the rest, as in Phase 15. Phase 15's 1 % stop
was a Phase 15 author decision; the Phase 16 spec sets no ceiling, so the count is recorded and
declared only (`units_over_window` called with `share=1.0`, its `stop` therefore false). At 29
of about 28,700 (0.1 %, derived) no ceiling would bind.
