# Spec Phase 13: choosing P1's bridge entity by the words around its mention

> Functional spec: WHAT is measured and WHY. The HOW goes in the plan (`13.0_*.md`).
> Status: **approved by the author on 2026-09-27 and frozen.** The direction (a per-entity score
> from a word window around each mention, with a cheap screen before the grid) and the open
> decisions (window width, sentence edges, masking and exclusion, several mentions, combination
> with rarity, number of seeds, the screen's reference rule and margin) were settled by the author
> on 2026-09-27, before any Phase 13 measurement. A change to any rule below is a written
> deviation `13.Y_*.md`. Plan: `13.0_bridge_window.md`.

---

## Problem and objective

**Problem.** P10-C (Dense + BM25 + the frozen Phase 9 Entity Hop, 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2) is the line's
best system: 66.50 % Full Support @2,048 on `test-10`, 4,801 / 7,405 on dev. Phases 11 and 12
tried to make the hop choose which of P1's entities to follow, and both stopped at their dev gate.
Phase 12 ranked P1's sentences by BGE similarity to the question and hopped only from the
entities of the best sentences; the best of its 396 dev configurations was P10-C's own point
(`phase_12/12.results.md`).

The Phase 12 closing diagnostic (post hoc, dev only, exploratory) looked at the **2,651** gold
paragraphs that Dense leaves outside its top 10 and that share at least one entity node with P1.
A gold paragraph's *bridge entities* are the P1 entity nodes it also holds.

- The sentence the ranking put first held a bridge entity for 67.6 % of the located cases, only
  about 4 points above the 63.7 % that always taking P1's first sentence reaches.
- Even when the sentence was right, the gold's rank did not improve (first 5: 368 -> 372): the
  sentence holds a median of 3 entities, and the bridge keeps competing with them.
- An oracle seeded only by each gold paragraph's bridge entities puts 1,131 of the 2,651 in the
  hop's first 5, against 590 for the all-entity hop. Choosing the entity is worth a lot.

A signal is therefore needed **per entity**, not per sentence. The words right around a mention
("a 1997 film directed by ___ and starring") carry the entity's role, which the question's
wording ("the director of") can match. Phase 12 rejected this window (its D1) because it adds a
width to fit; this phase fixes the width in advance instead.

**Question.**

> Does scoring each of P1's entities by the BGE similarity between the question and the words
> around its mention, and hopping only from the best-scored entities, retrieve the full
> multi-hop evidence more often than P10-C at FullWiki scale?

**Objective.** One candidate family, screened once on dev, then fitted on dev. The single
configuration dev selects is measured once on `test-11`:

- against **P10-C**, which decides the label;
- against **P10-B** (Dense + BM25), with its own preregistered test, because it is the reference
  the community understands.

If this phase fails, the next direction, already chosen by the author, is typed relations for P1's
entities only (roadmap 8.2).

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — The window score of an entity

- **Location and mentions.** As in Phase 12 D1, P1's sentences are its HotpotQA sentence list
  (the title is not a sentence), and an entity node's normalized form (normalization v1) is
  matched, bounded by non-word characters, in the normalized sentence text. Every match is a
  **mention**.
- **Words.** A normalized sentence is split on whitespace into words. The mention is the words
  that the match overlaps.
- **Window.** Up to **5 words before** the mention and up to **5 words after** it, joined by one
  space, left context first:
  - the width is fixed; there is no grid over it;
  - the window is **clipped at the sentence edges**: it never takes words from another sentence,
    and near an edge it is simply shorter;
  - the mention's own words are **masked**: they are left out of the window, which holds only
    the context. This keeps the entity the question names (for example *Titanic*) from winning
    by its name alone.
- **Empty windows.** A mention whose window is empty (a sentence made only of the mention) has no
  score. An entity located in no sentence, or whose windows are all empty, is **unscored**.
- **Similarity.** `sim(window) = cos(BGE(q), BGE(window))`:
  - `BGE(q)` is the question vector already cached for Dense, encoded exactly as Dense encodes
    questions (for BGE-small, with no instruction prefix);
  - `BGE(window)` encodes the window text as a passage with the Phase 9 Dense model and revision
    (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, L2-normalized). The window is taken from the normalized text, so
    it is lower-cased and has no diacritics.
- **Entity score.** `score(e)` is the **maximum** `sim` over the entity's scored mentions.
- **Entity ranking.** P1's scored entity nodes, by `score` descending, then rarity weight
  descending, then node id ascending.
- No exclusion of the entities the question names. No LLM, no trained component, no extra model.

### D2 — Seeds and the window-seeded hop

- **Seeds at `m`**, with `m ∈ {1, 2, 3, all}`:
  - for `m` an integer, the first `m` entities of the D1 ranking, or fewer if fewer are scored;
  - at `m = all`, every P1 entity node (`config.ENTITY_HOP_TYPES`), unscored ones included.
- **Candidates.** Paragraphs outside `read(q)` that share **at least one seed** with P1.
- **Candidate score.** The sum of rarity weights over **all** the entity nodes the candidate
  shares with P1, not only over the seeds. The seeds decide which paragraphs enter; the full
  overlap with P1 orders them. Under Phase 12's rule, a single seed would give every candidate
  the same score, leaving the order to the unit id.
- **Unchanged.** `read(q)` exclusion, positives only, ties by unit id, depth 100, no padding.
- **Empty seed set** (no scored entity at `m < all`): the hop list is empty.
- **Identity.** At `m = all` the seed filter admits every paragraph sharing any P1 entity, so the
  window-seeded hop is exactly the Phase 9 hop, and so P10-C's third component.

### D3 — The screen, before any list or fit

A cheap test of the signal itself, run once, before the dev lists, the reproduction and the grid.

- **Population.** The dev gold paragraphs that are outside the Phase 10 dev Dense top 10, are in
  the entity index, and share at least one entity node with P1: the 2,651 of the Phase 12
  diagnostic. The count is recomputed; if it differs from 2,651, the phase stops and goes to the
  author. Each gold paragraph is one item, so a question can contribute two.
- **Bridge set.** `bridge(g)` = P1's entity nodes ∩ `g`'s entity nodes.
- **Hit.** A rule hits item `g` when the entity it puts first is in `bridge(g)`. A question with
  no scored entity is a miss for every rule.
- **The three rules**, all choosing among the same set, P1's scored entities (D1):
  - **signal (W):** the first entity of the D1 ranking;
  - **first mention (T1):** the entity whose earliest mention comes first in P1: lowest sentence
    position, then earliest match offset in the normalized sentence, then node id;
  - **rarest (T2):** the highest rarity weight, then node id.
- **Reference:** `max(hit rate T1, hit rate T2)`.
- **Pass:** `hit rate W ≥ reference + 5.0 percentage points`. Otherwise the phase ends with
  **`SCREEN_STOP`**: no lists, no fit, `test-11` unopened.
- **Why +5.0 points.** Phase 12's sentence signal beat its own trivial rule (always P1's first
  sentence) by 3.9 points and bought nothing on dev. The screen must demand clearly more than a
  margin already shown to be insufficient.
- **What keeps it from being covert tuning:**
  - it evaluates exactly one configuration, the one D1 fixes; there is nothing to choose;
  - its output is go or stop; it selects no parameter used later;
  - it runs once, before any list is built, and is not repeated or altered after a failure;
  - `screen.json` is written by code and committed before the next stage runs.
- **Descriptive only** (they decide nothing): the hit rate of Phase 12's rule restated per entity
  (the first-mentioned entity of the sentence most similar to the question), and the share of
  items with a bridge among W's first 2 and first 3 entities.

### D4 — Laptop reproduction gate

Before the fit, the new code evaluated at `m = all`, `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2` must reproduce P10-C's dev
count **exactly: 4,801 / 7,405**, with 0 differing questions against the Phase 10 dev lists and a
hop list identical to Phase 10's for every dev question. A miss stops the phase and goes to the
author.

### D5 — The candidate family and the dev fit

P13 is `weighted` fusion with min-max normalization per component, the scheme P10-C used, over
three components in fixed order: Dense, BM25, the window-seeded hop at `m`.

- **Grid:** 4 values of `m` × the 66 weight triples of multiples of 0.1 that sum to 1, that is
  **264 configurations**.
- **The grid contains P10-C exactly:** `m = all`, `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2`.
- **Objective:** Full Support @2,048 over the 7,405 dev questions.
- **Ties** are broken by:
  1. gold recall @2,048;
  2. the larger `m` (`all` is the largest), the one closest to P10-C;
  3. the larger `w_dense`;
  4. the larger `w_bm25`.

### D6 — Dev gate

The selected configuration must reach **at least 4,834 / 7,405** dev Full Support @2,048, that is
P10-C's 4,801 plus 33 questions.

The margin rescales Phase 11's 37 questions for 1,430 configurations to this grid, since the
expected selection gain of the best of K noisy configurations grows as √(ln K):

`37 × √(ln 264 / ln 1,430) = 32.4`, rounded up to 33.

Below the bar, the phase stops with `DEV_STOP` and `test-11` stays unopened.

### D7 — Primary endpoint and label

Full Support @2,048 on `test-11`, P13 against P10-C: one two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial on
the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
BRIDGE_CHOICE_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
BRIDGE_CHOICE_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
BRIDGE_CHOICE_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DEV_STOP                     D6
SCREEN_STOP                  D3
```

The label is written by code. Nothing else can change it.

### D8 — Secondary comparison, preregistered

P13 against P10-B on the same questions and metric, with the same exact McNemar test, α = 0.05.
It is reported with wins, losses, delta and p, and it **cannot change the label**. The report
also gives P10-C against P10-B on `test-11` (descriptive): these are the first `test-11` figures of
either system.

### D9 — Latency, reported, not gated

Each system's `retrieve` wall time per question is measured in the same pass on the laptop, as in
Phase 10:

- it covers Dense scoring, BM25, the hop and fusion, and excludes the BGE question encoding;
- for P13, locating the mentions, building the windows and encoding them is new query-time work:
  on `test-11` it happens inside the pass, is **timed separately**, and is reported beside the
  `retrieve` figure and summed with it;
- the report gives mean, median, p95, max and the ratios P13 / P10-C and P13 / P10-B.

## User stories

### HU-1 — The window vectors on dev, computed once

**As** the author, **I want** every window of every dev P1 encoded once into its own cache,
**so that** the screen and the fit read fixed vectors.

- [ ] P1 is the first unit of the Phase 10 dev Dense lists, not recomputed.
- [ ] The vectors go to `data/phase13/cache/windows/`. The cache key covers the model, the
      revision, the window rule (5 words each side, sentence-clipped, mention masked, normalized
      text) and the set. No existing cache is overwritten.
- [ ] The run records window count, seconds and host.
- [ ] Coverage is recorded on dev: the share of P1 entity nodes scored, the mentions per entity,
      the empty-window count, and the share of questions with no scored entity.

**Projection, not a measurement.** Phase 12 located 45,852 P1 entity nodes on dev and encoded
22,444 sentences in 656.6 s on the laptop. With one window per mention, the planning figures are
about 50,000-60,000 windows, 15-30 min of encoding (windows are shorter than sentences), plus the
roughly 20 min that reading the FullWiki corpus for P1's sentences took in Phase 12, and about
85 MB of vectors.

## Restrictions

- **R1.** No rented compute and no paid API. Everything runs on the laptop.
- **R2.** Phase 9-12 artifacts, dev lists and caches are read-only. Phase 13 writes only under
  `data/phase13/`.
- **R3.** Nothing is fitted or selected on `probe`, `test-10` or `test-11`. `test-10` is spent: it
  is neither retrieved on nor read again, not even descriptively.
- **R4.** P10-B and P10-C keep their frozen weights. The hop keeps `read(q)`, its rarity weight,
  depth 100 and the tie-break by unit id.
- **R5.** Window vectors use the Phase 9 Dense model and revision. Any other encoder is a different
  phase.

## Anti-objectives

Phase 13 does **not**:

- re-extract the corpus, rebuild the entity index or change the normalization;
- canonicalize entities, link them to titles or articles, use hyperlinks or entity types;
- add relations, a second hop, seeds outside P1, several P1s or graph propagation;
- re-rank or filter hop candidates by their own relevance to the question;
- fit the window width, weight seeds softly, add a temperature or learn anything;
- exclude the entities the question names;
- run GLiNER, an LLM or a cross-encoder at query time;
- refit P10-B or P10-C, retry the screen with another window after a `SCREEN_STOP`, or try a
  second candidate family after a `DEV_STOP`.

## Limitations, declared in advance

1. **Dev has been fitted by Phases 10, 11 and 12.** The 7,405 validation questions remain valid
   for selection, not as held-out evidence.
2. **The screen's population and its bridge definition come from Phase 12's post-hoc
   diagnostic** on the same dev questions. The screen tests one fixed signal against it and
   chooses nothing, but the population was not defined blind.
3. **The bridge set is lenient.** Any P1 entity the gold paragraph also holds counts, including
   generic ones, so a hit is not proof that the entity is the one the question needs.
4. **The oracle's 1,131 is optimistic for small `m`**: each gold paragraph holds all its bridge
   entities by construction, so it is not the ceiling of this family.
5. **Windows are taken from normalized text** (lower-cased, no diacritics, separators as
   spaces). BGE-small's tokenizer lower-cases anyway; the rest was not measured.
6. **A window around one mention also covers its neighbours.** Two entities in the same short
   clause can get similar scores; the screen measures how much this matters.
