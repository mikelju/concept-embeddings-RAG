# Phase 4 results: navigating the corpus by diffusion over `X`

> Produced by T17. Every figure below is read from an on-disk artifact named by the configuration
> that produced it. Nothing here is recomputed for the report.

## Verdict

**The expansion does not reach a second paragraph, and the reason is arithmetic rather than
tuning.** System C at the frozen configuration scores **0.8214 Full Support on test at 2,048
tokens** against dense's 0.8250 and the dense+BM25 control's 0.8643. It is below the bar this phase
declared before measuring anything, and below the baseline it was meant to improve on.

**The mechanism is measured, not inferred.** At every restart in the declared grid, the walk returns
**exactly the seed's 100 units, reordered** - zero new units, on every dev question sampled. The
diffusion reaches all 19,366 units on every question (a flood, not a hop) and therefore gives each
non-seed unit a share too small to clear the floor the restart holds under the seed's own hits. The
numbers are in §"Why no new unit is ever promoted": the lightest seed unit sits at 0.00761 of the
mass and the heaviest non-seed unit can reach 0.00021. A factor of 36.

**So System C is a re-ranker of dense's top-100, and the 2x2 says what re-ranking bought**: it gains
5 dev questions dense misses and loses 6 dense had, for 486 against dense's 487. It recovers **3 of
the 72** that defeat both baselines - System B recovered 0 and the control recovers 6 - and **one of
those three is recovered by nothing else in this project**.

**The restart is doing exactly what §52-§53 said it would, in both directions.** At `restart = 0.0`
the walk is query-independent by construction and collapses to **0.0117 Full Support** - the
declared failure mode, now a number. At the restart fractions that keep it query-aware, the same
term pins the ranking to the seed. The grid found no point between the two, and the arithmetic says
why: the crossover where a new unit could enter sits at **restart < 0.10**, below the declared grid's
floor of 0.2. That is a gap this phase did not measure, declared as one rather than filled.

**A negative verdict, and an attributable one.** The concept space is not being blamed for a bad
implementation: the operator is checked against hand arithmetic, `restart = 1.0` reproduces the seed
exactly, and the two seed arms differ in the seed and in nothing else. What failed is the method as
specified, on this corpus, at this scale.

## Why no new unit is ever promoted

This is the finding of the phase, and it is arithmetic rather than opinion. Measured over the first
120 dev questions at the chosen normalization:

| restart | New units in the final top-100 | Overlap of the final top-16 with the seed's |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.2 | **0.00** | 13.40 of 16 |
| 0.4 | **0.00** | 13.98 of 16 |
| 0.6 | **0.00** | 14.67 of 16 |
| 0.8 | **0.00** | 15.24 of 16 |

**At every restart in the declared grid, the walk returns exactly the seed's 100 units, reordered.**
Not approximately: zero new units, on every question sampled, in all four cells.

The reason is in three measurements:

- **The walk floods rather than hops.** All **19,366** units carry positive mass on every question,
  from the first round. 19.0% of the final mass sits outside the seed's top-100 - spread across
  19,266 units, which is about a hundred-thousandth each.
- **The seed's mass is nearly flat.** Dense cosines on this corpus sit in a narrow band, so after
  L1-normalization the lightest of the 100 seed units holds 0.00951 and the heaviest 0.01320 -
  against 0.01 for a uniform split. The 100th hit is nearly as heavy as the first.
- **The propagation never concentrates.** The heaviest single unit after one round holds 0.00119 of
  the propagated mass, and the heaviest **non-seed** unit 0.00104. At K = 512 the largest concept
  covers 7.2% of the pool (Phase 2), so a concept hands its mass to well over a thousand units.

Put together, at `restart = 0.8` the lightest seed unit is floored at `0.8 x 0.00951 = 0.00761`
while the best a non-seed unit can reach is `0.2 x 0.00104 = 0.00021`. **A factor of 36.** The
crossover - where a non-seed unit could out-score the weakest seed unit - is at

```
(1 - r) x 0.00104 > r x 0.00951    ->    r < 0.0986
```

**below the declared grid's floor of 0.2.** The grid could not have found the regime where the
expansion promotes a new unit, and it was fixed before any of this was known.

**That regime may not be reachable either.** `restart = 0.0`, the one point measured below the grid,
is the collapse §52-§53 predicted: 0.0117 Full Support, 0.0400 gold recall, the walk running to the
cap on all 600 questions in both arms. Whether anything survives between 0 and 0.10 is **a declared
gap of this phase, not a filled one**, and the obvious first ablation for Phase 5.

| The owner of Straight Blast Gym Ireland's birthday? | `c22` *Combat sports fighters* + `c14` *Major Boxing Superfights* | **Yes** - a biography and combat sports |

Three of these are the same questions Phase 3 read, arrived at by a different mechanism. **The shape
Phase 3 named was real and it reproduces.** The concept space does identify a paragraph that is
about two subjects at once, which is the shape of a bridge.

**On the dense arm it is weaker.** Of the five questions System C gains over dense, two are clean
conjunctions - a baseball player *and* a Hall of Fame anthology (`c439` + `c183`), a conservatory
teacher *and* a composer (`c436` + `c505`) - and three are not: a Simpsons episode, a legal
settlement, the medieval genealogy above. Started from dense, the re-ranking moves questions for
reasons that are not all conjunctive.

**What the hypothesis does not buy.** The signal is real, it is reproducible, and it is worth about
four questions in 600. The method built to convert it into retrieval - diffusion with restart -
cannot, because at any restart that keeps it query-aware the new paragraph never out-scores the
seed's hundredth hit.

Traces were produced for **80 dev questions** under the sampling rule D10 fixed before the run: the

**Three ablations this phase earned rather than guessed:**

1. **`restart` between 0 and 0.10**, the regime where the arithmetic says a new unit could enter and
   which the declared grid could not reach. Bounded below by the `restart = 0.0` collapse measured
   here.
2. **Concept pruning**, still a declared Phase 5 ablation and now with a reason: the concepts
   carrying the diffusion are the broad ones, and the traces name them.
3. **A seed deeper than 100**, or a candidate set that excludes the seed, since the finding is that
   the seed's own mass floor is what blocks promotion.

the corpus would have to produce without annotation - remains the declared gap of the master plan.
