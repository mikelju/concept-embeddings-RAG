# Deviation 17.1: CLM-8B, an open decision model, as a third declared judge

> Status: **decided by the author on 2026-10-01, before any pair was scored.** Nothing of Phase 17
> has been implemented or measured, so declaring a third judge now adds a candidate in advance;
> it is not a retry after seeing figures. The spec's D2 ("two judges, no third") is corrected by
> this deviation in its own commit; the plan is propagated.

## Context

Between the plan's approval and the start of `/6-implementar`, the author raised Jev, TypeSafe
AI's "System One" decision model (released 2026-09-15), as a possible judge and a differentiation
for the paper. The check of 2026-10-01 found Jev to be API-only, closed, waitlisted and without
retrieval benchmarks, and found an open model of the same class built for ranking text
candidates against a question: **CLM-8B**.

## What CLM-8B is (verified 2026-10-01 on its model card and repository)

- `Contrastive-LM/CLM-v0.1-8B`, Apache 2.0 code and weights; authors Kwok, Kang, Suresh,
  Saad-Falcon, Pavone, Ré and Mirhoseini (Stanford groups; the card states no affiliation).
- A frozen `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` encoder with two projection heads of about 20 M parameters, trained
  with a bidirectional InfoNCE objective on about 60 M Nemotron question-answer pairs, about
  30 M synthetic hard negatives and about 1 M agentic trajectories.
- **A bi-encoder:** the question ("state") and each candidate are embedded separately; the score
  is their similarity; `engine.rank(question, candidates)` returns every candidate with a
  probability (a softmax over the candidates given). Served locally with vLLM
  (`pip install contrastive-lm`); states beyond 2,048 tokens are truncated.
- No passage-retrieval benchmark is published for it; its parity with Jev was measured on agent
  tasks. Whether it judges evidence well is the open question this deviation lets Phase 17
  measure.

## Decision

**J-decision = CLM-8B joins J-light and J-strong as a third declared judge**, under the spec's
rules for judges, with these particulars:

1. **Identity.** `Contrastive-LM/CLM-v0.1-8B` and its encoder `Qwen/Qwen3-8B`, both pinned by
   revision at download, the heads' file SHA-256 recorded, library versions recorded.
2. **Input.** The state is the question text alone, no instruction; each candidate is the unit's
   `indexable_text`, as for the other judges. Encoder truncation at its own limit, counted.
3. **Score.** The pre-softmax similarity the library exposes; if it exposes only the probability,
   the probability, recorded which. Ordering as D1: inside each system's own top-100, ties by
   original rank; the union line as the spec says.
4. **Precision.** The encoder runs in bfloat16, the dtype its weights are stored in; an RTX 4090
   cannot hold Qwen3-8B in float32. Recorded, with the determinism re-score of the plan.
5. **Each unit is embedded once per set** and reused across the questions that list it, which is
   what the architecture allows; the per-question cost at query time is reported as the
   question's embedding plus the similarities against pre-embedded candidates, and the offline
   cost per unit beside it.
6. **Order on the pod:** J-light, J-strong, then J-decision, so the two cheap judges are secured
   first; within J-decision, MultiHop-RAG, MuSiQue, then HotpotQA; throughput measured on the
   first shard and the session projected before HotpotQA starts.
7. **Comparisons (D5).** The five preregistered comparisons per judge apply to J-decision too;
   the cross-judge comparisons are J-strong(P10-B) against J-light(P10-B), as before, and
   J-decision(P10-B) against J-strong(P10-B). Labels per judge as the spec writes them.
8. **Dependencies.** `contrastive-lm` and `vllm` in an optional dependency group for the pod
   only (Linux x86_64), never synced on the ARM64 laptop, authorized by the author with this
   deviation.
9. **Cost cap.** The pod session's stop rises from 5 to **10 USD**, authorized by the author with
   this deviation. Projection for J-decision alone: 2-8 h on an RTX 4090, 1.5-6 USD (wide, no
   published throughput; the first shard measures it).
10. **Jev stays out of Phase 17.** Closed, waitlisted and mutable; it may be compared in the
    successor project as an API reference if access arrives.
11. **Fourth judge: none**, whatever the figures.

## Why

The author wants the paper to carry a differentiation beyond the two cross-encoders. A decision
model of a new class, open and reproducible, measured for the first time as an evidence judge
against specialized rerankers, with its cost, is that differentiation whichever way it comes out.
It also tells the successor project cheaply whether CLM-8B's relevance judgments are good enough
to try it as a first-stage retriever over a whole corpus.
