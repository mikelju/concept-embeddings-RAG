> Functional spec: [`17.spec.md`](17.spec.md), approved 2026-09-30 and frozen. This plan decides
> HOW; it changes no spec rule and adds no threshold, gate, outcome, judge or variant. A change to
> any spec rule is a written deviation `17.Y_*.md`.
>
> Status (2026-10-01): **approved by the author on 2026-10-01**, with the four decisions recorded in
> the last section. Implemented on the working branch `phase-17-judge-paper` by `/6-implementar`.
>
> Precedent: Phase 16 ([plan](../phase_16/16.0_multihop_rag_validation.md),
> [recipe](../phase_16/16.runpod_recipe.md), [results](../phase_16/16.results.md)); its condition
> stands: **reuse the code of the earlier phases**, generalizing a function with defaults that
> leave its callers unchanged, never copying it. One question, **eight steps**, no `17.tasks.md`;
> code for a step past a stop state is not written.
>
> Where things run: a **rented RunPod GPU** for the judges' scoring only (R1), created, driven over
> SSH and terminated by the agent through the RunPod GraphQL API, as in Phase 16; the **laptop**
> for everything else. Projected rented cost **1.5-2.5 USD** for a 2.0-3.4 h session (projection;
> basis under "Numbers and projections"); the session stops at **5 USD** (D8).

## Objective

> With every retriever frozen and nothing fitted, how much of that room does a zero-shot judge
> close on each corpus, at what cost, and does the Entity Hop still add evidence a judge can use?

J-light (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) and J-strong (`BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`) each
score once every pair of the union of the four frozen systems' fused top-100 on HotpotQA dev
(7,405), MuSiQue validation (2,417) and MultiHop-RAG answerable (2,255); each system is reordered
inside its own top-100, the union pool is a fifth, descriptive line (D1). D5 and D6 are written by
code, with no gate; then D7, the paper (D9) and the venues (D10). The only terminal states are
`DATA_STOP` (D3) and a cost stop (D8).

## Author stop points

1. **Before renting the GPU (end of S3).** The recipe, the rate, the frozen commit, the uploads
   (bytes and SHA-256), the projected cost and the open decisions (last section) are presented;
   no money is spent before the author says go.
2. **`DATA_STOP` or a reproduction miss (S2).** A D3 figure not reproduced writes `DATA_STOP`. A
   refusal of the reused readers, or a miss of the four replays recorded beside D3, stops the
   stage for the author without a label (`CLAUDE.md`: a reproduction check that misses).
3. **On the pod.** The session reaches **5 USD** (D8), or the projection made after J-strong's
   first shard exceeds it; a served revision or weights digest other than the pin; any refusal:
   the pod is stopped and the author asked.
4. **ASK FIRST.** A new dependency (none expected) or a system tool (the TeX engine, asked at stop
   point 1 so that S7 does not wait).
5. **The venue and any submission** are the author's (S7).
6. Two attempts without progress.

## Technical notes

### The judges: pins, loading, precision, determinism

| | J-light | J-strong |
|---|---|---|
| Model | `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`, `BertForSequenceClassification`, 6 layers, hidden 384; 22.7 M parameters (derived from 90,870,598 bytes of float32 weights) | `BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`, `XLMRobertaForSequenceClassification`, 24 layers, hidden 1,024; 567.8 M parameters (derived from 2,271,071,852 bytes; its config says float32) |
| Hub head on 2026-10-01 (metadata, measured) | revision `233902d2…ee0a`, `model.safetensors` SHA-256 `821d1aa6…b0ae`, `tokenizer.json` served | revision `953dc6f6…d41e`, `model.safetensors` SHA-256 `d9e3e081…5286`, `tokenizer.json` served |
| Own maximum length (tokenizer config at that revision) | 512 | 8,192 (`max_position_embeddings` 8,194) |
| Batch on the pod | 256 pairs | 32 pairs |

- **No new package (expected).** `sentence-transformers` 6.0.1 (main group, locked) provides
  `CrossEncoder`, which loads any `*ForSequenceClassification` checkpoint with `revision`,
  `max_length`, `activation_fn` and `model_kwargs`. Both pins serve `tokenizer.json`, so the fast
  tokenizers need neither `sentencepiece` nor `protobuf` (locked only in the `phase7` group, which
  the pod does not sync: a hidden need fails at load, before any pair). A needed package goes to
  the author (ASK FIRST), never assumed.
- **Loading.** `CrossEncoder(name, revision=pin, activation_fn=torch.nn.Identity())`, float32
  weights and safetensors only through `model_kwargs`, no `trust_remote_code`, the maximum length
  left to the model. Before any pair, the served snapshot's name and its `model.safetensors`
  SHA-256 (`embeddings.backend.snapshot_directory`, `weights_sha256`) must equal the pin.
- **The score is the raw logit.** The sigmoid is monotone, so it would not change the order, but
  in float32 it saturates near 1 for large logits and would manufacture ties; the logit is the
  model's own score, uncalibrated (D2).
- **Precision: float32 (proposal).** The judge's order is the whole variant. Float16 logits carry
  about three significant digits, so near-equal candidates would tie and the tie-break, not the
  judge, would order them. Float32 is the precision both checkpoints are stored in; matmuls stay
  at `highest` (no TF32), recorded. The cost difference is under 1 USD (projection). Float16 is
  the alternative for the author, with its caveat recorded.
- **Truncation.** Each pair is also tokenized untruncated by the judge's own tokenizer, special
  tokens included; longer than the resolved maximum counts as truncated (per set: count, share,
  longest). The judge truncates as its tokenizer does for pairs, recorded.
- **Determinism (D2).** The shard (250 questions, one `predict` call; `CrossEncoder` sorts by
  length inside it) is the unit of batch composition, so a resumed run scores every shard in the
  same composition. After each set's last shard, the pairs of its first 20 questions are re-scored
  at batch size 1: the largest absolute difference and the number of those questions whose order
  changes are recorded, deciding nothing.
- **Manifest (`scoring-<judge>.json`).** Name, revision requested and served, weights SHA-256,
  parameters, maximum length, dtype, matmul precision, activation, batch, library versions
  (torch, transformers, sentence-transformers, tokenizers, huggingface-hub, safetensors), GPU,
  host and container allocation (the Phase 16 trap), commit, rate. Per set, a block added once by
  that set's process: questions, pairs, truncations, shards, restarts, wall seconds (load apart),
  pairs/s, USD, peak VRAM and RSS, determinism, pairs SHA-256 read, scores SHA-256 and digest.

### The pool, the pairs and the scores

- `pool-<set>.jsonl.gz`: one line per question, in the set's recorded order:
  `{"qid", "units": [[unit_id, {"<system>": rank, ...}], ...]}`, 1-based ranks in each system's
  fused top-100, units ordered by best rank over the four systems, then unit id.
- `pairs-<set>.jsonl.gz` (the pod's only input; recorded by digest, never committed):
  `{"qid", "question", "unit_ids", "texts"}` in pool order, `texts` the units' `indexable_text`.
  One self-contained line per question is exactly what gets scored; the FullWiki corpus is never
  uploaded. Texts come from `fullwiki.load_corpus`, not a new streaming reader: it proves every
  id and the order, and Phase 16's D4 measured the same HotpotQA load at a 4,160 MB peak.
- `scores-<judge>-<set>.jsonl.gz`: `{"qid", "unit_ids", "scores"}` in the pairs file's order.
- `pool.json`: per set, questions, pool size (mean, median, p90, min, max), pairs, units listed by
  1-4 systems, the longest pool unit in budget tokens, every source's SHA-256 and recorded
  digest, the pool and pairs files' SHA-256, digest and bytes; the judges' pins; code commit.
- `pairs-<set>` and `published-figures.json` add to the spec's data contract, changing none of it.

### The reorder rule and the union line (D1)

- **J(S)**: S's fused top-100, sorted by the judge's score, descending, ties broken by S's
  original rank. Nothing outside S's top-100 enters, so the list is a permutation: FS@100 of J(S)
  equals S's, checked by the stage.
- **The union under J**: every pool unit by score, descending, ties broken by best original rank
  over the four systems, then unit id (the pool's own order). Written whole; measured at depth
  100 like every list of the line (a 4,096-token context holds about 40-61 units at 66.7-101.8
  budget tokens per unit, derived); its ceiling uses the whole pool. Descriptive, in no D5
  comparison.
- `reordered-<judge>-<set>-<line>.jsonl.gz`: `{"qid", "ranked": [[unit_id, score, rank]]}`, the
  last field the original (or best) rank, so every tie-break is auditable.

### D5 and D6

- **Records.** Every list is replayed through `_p10_replay_records` (`phase10.ReplayRetriever`
  into `phase9.measure_system`, the Phase 1 harness): budgets 512-4,096 with Full Support, gold
  recall and context precision; `fs_at_k` and `gpr_at_k` at 2-20. The recorded outcomes of
  Phases 15 and 16 are not reused as control records, so both sides of every comparison come
  through one route.
- **D5.** `phase15.paired(control, candidate)` gives n, wins, losses, ties, delta and the exact
  two-sided p; eleven comparisons per set (five per judge, plus J-strong(P10-B) against
  J-light(P10-B)). The label comes from comparison 1 only, through `phase14.label(...,
  supported="HOP_ADDS_UNDER_JUDGE", regression="HOP_HURTS_UNDER_JUDGE",
  not_supported="HOP_NEUTRAL_UNDER_JUDGE")`. HotpotQA's block carries
  `in_sample_weights: true` (D3).
- **D6.** `phase17.line_metrics`: gold recall and full support at k units (`metrics.recall_at_k`,
  `metrics.full_support`), averaged over questions, and nDCG@10 (`metrics.ndcg_at_k`); for
  k ≤ 20 they must equal the harness's values (checked). The ceiling: questions with every gold
  unit in S's top-100, and in the union pool, and the Full Support failures at 2,048 inside it;
  the spec's ceilings (1,160 / 1,145 / 1,298; 900 / 1,317 / 1,410) and its 932 failures for P10-C
  on HotpotQA dev are recomputed and compared in the results.

### The paper: toolchain and R5

- **No TeX engine on the laptop** (checked 2026-10-01: `latexmk`, `pdflatex`, `xelatex`,
  `lualatex`, `tectonic`, `pandoc` not on PATH; Docker, WSL and winget present). **Proposal:
  Tectonic**, one self-contained binary (its x86-64 Windows build runs under Windows 11's
  emulation) that fetches only the packages the document uses: a system tool, so the author's
  decision. Alternatives: TeX Live with `latexmk` inside WSL (larger); or no engine, with the
  Markdown copies below and the PDF built by the author elsewhere (C7 then checked by the author).
- **R5.** `cer p17-tables` writes `tables/*.tex` (tabular bodies); `tables/numbers.tex`, one
  macro per number the prose cites, each commented with its artifact path, SHA-256 prefix and
  JSON key; `figures/*.tex`, pgfplots with written coordinates (matplotlib is not a dependency);
  `tables/*.md`. Sources: the committed Phase 17 JSONs, `published-figures.json`, and earlier
  artifacts read-only (Phases 9, 10, 14 outcome files are not in git; their SHA-256 is checkable
  against their results). The generated files are committed: the PDF builds from the repository.
- **Form.** A venue-neutral two-column `article`, about 8 pages plus references; the chosen
  venue's class replaces the preamble later. An `\ifanonymous` switch drops the name, repository
  URL and acknowledgements for double-blind venues (the mirror is the author's, at submission).
  Sole author, no affiliation; AI assistance disclosed per the venue.
- **Outline.** Introduction and position (untrained, no LLM at query time, cheap); the Entity Hop
  and its indexing cost; setup; results at FullWiki scale (9-14), in transfer (15-16) and under
  the judges (17), cost beside every figure; published figures (D7); the concept-space negative
  result (2-4), short; limitations (at least the spec's six); conclusion; reproducibility appendix.

- **Rule.** A figure enters only after it is read in the paper itself (arXiv, ACL Anthology,

  published, value, split and question count, corpus and size, unit, training or LLM use, a note
  on every difference from ours, verification date. Unverifiable means excluded. Every caption
  says "published figures, not a paired comparison".

## Decisions taken by the author (2026-10-01)

1. **Precision on the pod: float32.** Float16 was rejected: its three significant digits would
   make near-equal candidates tie, and the tie-break, not the judge, would order them.
2. **TeX engine: Tectonic**, installed only after its compatibility with the Windows ARM64 laptop
   is checked (a native `aarch64-pc-windows-msvc` build if one exists, otherwise the x86-64 build
   under Windows 11's emulation, recorded either way). If neither runs, fall back to the Markdown
   copies of the tables and build the PDF elsewhere, and say so in the results.
3. **The four replays outside D3** (P10-A on the three sets, P10-B on HotpotQA dev) are recorded;
   a miss stops the stage for the author without a label. No terminal state is added.
4. **Dependencies:** none expected. If the pod's load of either judge needs `sentencepiece` or
   `protobuf`, the author has authorized adding them to the main group in advance; the addition
   is recorded in the plan and the results.
