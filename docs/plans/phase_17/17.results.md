# Phase 17 results: three zero-shot judges over the frozen systems, and the paper

> Phase 17 is **measured and closed on 2026-10-01** (spec [`17.spec.md`](17.spec.md), plan
> [`17.0_judge_and_paper.md`](17.0_judge_and_paper.md), deviation
> [`17.1_clm8b_third_judge.md`](17.1_clm8b_third_judge.md), recipe
> [`17.runpod_recipe.md`](17.runpod_recipe.md)). No terminal state was reached: `DATA_STOP` (D3)
> did not occur and no cost stop was hit. The phase has no gate; the labels below are written by
> code (D5). Nothing was fitted, selected, generated or trained, and `test-11` was not opened.
>
> **Labels.** Every value is one of: *measured* (read off an artifact or a command), *derived*
> (arithmetic on measured values), *exploratory* (computed from the gold, a diagnostic, never a
> result), *projection* (neither measured nor derived), *interpretation* (a reading, not tested).
> The tables copy `docs/paper/tables/*.md`, written by `cer p17-tables` from the artifacts, and
> carry the same numbers as the paper; unless a row says otherwise their cells are *measured*.
> Digests are shortened to their first eight and last four characters; the full values are in the
> named files.

## The headline

With every retriever frozen, nothing fitted and every judge used zero-shot, **J-strong
(`BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`) lifts every system on every set**. Full Support @2,048 of the standard
recipe P10-B goes from 4,536 to 5,198 of 7,405 on HotpotQA dev, from 524 to 704 of 2,417 on
MuSiQue and from 587 to 822 of 2,255 on MultiHop-RAG (derived from `outcome.json`; +8.94, +7.45
and +10.42 pp, exact McNemar p below 1e-28 on each). The hop's pool still adds evidence under
J-light and J-strong on HotpotQA and MuSiQue (`HOP_ADDS_UNDER_JUDGE` four times out of four) and
**hurts on MultiHop-RAG under J-strong** (`HOP_HURTS_UNDER_JUDGE`, 24 wins / 49 losses). J-light
(`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) **hurts every system on news** (P10-B −10.42 pp; P10-A's
−1.24 pp is not significant) while helping P10-A and P10-B on the two Wikipedia benchmarks and
lowering P14 on both. J-decision (CLM-8B, a
bi-encoder decision model) **hurts every system on every set** (P10-B −35.84, −14.48 and −16.81 pp):
a negative result for a decision model used as an evidence judge, zero-shot with the question
alone as its state. P14 under J-strong reaches the union pool's level on HotpotQA (5,911 against
5,922, in-sample for P14's weights) and exceeds it on MuSiQue (871 against 809); the union pool
under J-strong leads on MultiHop-RAG (846 against 822 for P10-B, the best single system there).
The three judges cost 3.28 USD of attributable GPU time (derived); the rented session cost
5.2325 USD by the account balance; the invoice is pending. A 25-page paper exists and builds from
the repository. All of this is *measured* or *derived* from `outcome.json`, `metrics.json` and the
three `scoring-<judge>.json`; section 12 holds the interpretation.

## 3. The judges

All three are declared in advance, used zero-shot (no fine-tuning, threshold, calibration, prompt
beyond the pair, or fusion with the first-stage score), reported everywhere, with no selection
between them and no fourth (D2, deviation 17.1). The class difference is declared: J-light and
J-strong read the question and one candidate together; J-decision embeds the question (its
*state*, no instruction) and each unit (its *candidate*) separately and scores their similarity.
Pins were read from the Hub on 2026-10-01 and each served revision and weights SHA-256 equalled
its pin on the pod. All *measured* unless marked.

**Found during the phase (all *measured* unless marked).**

1. **J-decision is not deterministic under batching.** Re-scoring the first 20 questions of each
   set with one text per request against 32 gave a largest absolute difference of 0.335 / 0.386 /
   0.373 logits (MultiHop-RAG / MuSiQue / HotpotQA, scale 100), and **all 20 questions reorder on
   every set** (the encoder is bfloat16 and vLLM schedules sequences into GPU batches itself). The
   cross-encoders gave 7e-6 to 6e-5 and 0 reorders. J-decision's figures are one realization of the
   model; how much a second pass would move Full Support was not measured. Recorded as a finding,
   not a gate (plan D2).
2. **The 74-token dedup note.** MuSiQue's J-decision manifest reports 52,244 state tokens against
   52,318 expected: the library's `Embedder` deduplicates five repeated question texts inside a
   250-question shard (verified on the laptop with the pinned tokenizer). Same text, same
   embedding; no score is affected. The other two sets match exactly. Harmless.
3. **Mixed CUDA runtime.** On the pod the lock installs a CUDA 13 runtime for vLLM next to torch
   `2.13.0+cu126`. `import vllm` failed on the loader path (fixed in the pod's `env.sh`, no
   dependency change) and `vllm serve` failed on PyPI's CUDA 13 torchvision (corrective `6aa111b`).
   After that, a cross-check of vLLM's embeddings against a transformers CPU bfloat16 pass of the
   same pinned Qwen3-8B gave cosines 0.99983 / 0.99997 / 0.99995 (last token, L2-normalized;
   cross-text cosine 0.78): the mixed runtime did not corrupt the vectors to that precision. The
   check decides nothing.
4. **Truncation.** J-light truncated 731 of 317,776 MultiHop-RAG pairs (0.23 %; longest 1,726
   tokens), 42 of 400,745 on MuSiQue and 65 of 1,295,224 on HotpotQA; J-strong and J-decision
   truncated none. Whether 731 pairs can explain J-light's −10.42 pp on news (324 questions lost
   against 89 won) was not tested; at 0.23 % of pairs it is unlikely to be the main cause unless
   the truncated pairs concentrate among gold units (*interpretation*, untested).
5. **Manifest gap.** `allocation.cgroup_*` are null in the three manifests; the recipe holds the
   values. A prospective fix in the producer, not an edit of the manifests.
6. **Two environment failures** on the pod (CUDA runtime library, torchvision build) cost about
   40 min of the session; neither is an out-of-memory event and `restarts` is 0.

## 12. Interpretation, and the next research decision

Everything in this section is an *interpretation* unless marked; none of it was tested.

1. **The standard recipe was a weak control, and the gains of Phases 14 to 16 must be read against
   J-strong(P10-B).** Without a judge P14 reaches 5,224 on HotpotQA (in-sample), 761 on MuSiQue and
   513 on MultiHop-RAG; J-strong(P10-B) reaches 5,198, 704 and 822 (*derived* from section 1).
   On HotpotQA P14 without a judge is level with the standard recipe under J-strong (5,224 against
   5,198, in-sample for P14), on MuSiQue it is ahead of it (761 against 704), and on news the
   judge's step (+235 questions) is far larger than the hop's, which was negative. The paper
   therefore does not claim that the hop beats a judge-equipped standard recipe.
2. **The hop's role is candidate generation, on Wikipedia.** Under J-light and J-strong the hop's
   pool still carries evidence a judge can use on HotpotQA and MuSiQue (J(P14) against J(P10-B):
   +9.63 and +6.91 pp under J-strong), and P14's pool is the larger one on both (ceilings 6,119 and
   1,317 against 5,267 and 900 for P10-B, *exploratory*). On HotpotQA P14 under J-strong sits at the
   union pool's level, so its pool is already the best of the four; on MuSiQue it exceeds the union
   (871 against 809), which a hypothesis reads as a larger pool letting a judge be wrong more
   often. On news the reading reverses: P10-B's pool holds more gold than P14's (1,160 against
   1,145) and J(P14) loses to J(P10-B) under J-strong and J-decision. It is a hypothesis
   supported on Wikipedia and not on news, the same boundary Phase 16 left.
3. **J-light's news regression is unexplained.** It lowers every system on news (P10-B −10.42 pp,
   324 questions lost against 89 won), where J-strong gains 10.42 pp on the same pools. The two
   differ in size, training data and maximum length; truncation (0.23 % of pairs) is unlikely to be
   the main cause alone (section 11, found 4), and nothing else was tested. What the cheap judge
   does is to make the *standard recipe* worse on news, the corpus where BM25 carries the evidence;
   one reading is that J-light's order discards what the lexical component found, which this phase
   did not test.
4. **J-decision's failure is a measurement of this use of the model, not of the model.** It
   lowers every system by 7.58 to 45.02 pp, wins 12 and loses 3,328 questions against J-strong(P10-B) on
   HotpotQA, and its hop labels compare two bad orders. It is a bi-encoder with no published
   passage-retrieval benchmark, built for agent decisions, and was used with the question alone as
   its state and no instruction (the preregistration forbade another prompt). "It does not rank
   evidence" and "this usage does not suit it" are not separable here. It also reorders under
   batching (found 1). For the successor project this means CLM-8B is not a ready first-stage or
   rerank component under this protocol; a different state (question plus the first paragraph, an
   instruction) is a declared candidate in a new experiment, not a retry of this one.
5. **For the successor project (the author's decision).** (a) The union pool under J-strong is the
   natural reference: 5,922 / 7,405, 809 / 2,417 and 846 / 2,255 (79.97, 33.47 and 37.52 %,
   descriptive, *measured*). It is the best pool any of the four systems can offer, at the price of
   about 141 to 175 judge pairs per question (about 0.45 to 0.54 s per question on one RTX 4090
   for J-strong, *derived*, section 4). (b) The Entity Hop as one candidate generator among others,
   with its indexing and query cost beside every result, rather than as the line's final system.
   (c) The ceiling, not the order, is now the larger gap on MuSiQue and news: 41.7 % and 42.4 % of
   questions have gold outside the union pool, and under J-strong the union pool still fails 601
   and 452 questions inside its ceiling (*derived*), so better retrieval and better judging are
   both open.
6. **No selection happened anywhere in the phase.** All three judges were reported on all three
   sets, no fourth judge, no threshold or calibration, no fusion of a judge's score with the first
   stage, no weight or `alpha` changed; the labels are written by code from comparison 1; the
   union line is descriptive and in no preregistered comparison. `test-11` is spent and was not
   opened; no new corpus was opened. HotpotQA dev, the dev set of the line, is in-sample for the
   weights of P10-C and P14 and is marked so wherever it appears.
7. **What remains with the author.** The venue and any submission (`venues.md`: ARR October 2026
   closes 12 October 2026 AoE; arXiv needs an endorsement); the author's full name in `main.tex`;
   the invoice, to be copied from Billing; and the choice of the next step with the successor
   project charter in view.
