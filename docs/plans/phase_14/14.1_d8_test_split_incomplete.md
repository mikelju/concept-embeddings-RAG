# Deviation 14.1: the D8 split by Dense rank cannot be computed on `test-11`

> Status: **recorded 2026-09-29.** Found at S6, after the single `test-11` pass (S5) and before
> `outcome.json` was written. It changes nothing that decides the label. Whether anything more is
> wanted is the author's decision.

## Context

Spec D8 ("Overlap with Dense, reported, deciding nothing") asks, on dev for the selected
configuration **and on `test-11` for P14 against P10-C**, that every gold paragraph whose coverage
within the 2,048-token budget changed in a won or lost question be classified by its Dense rank:
1-10, 11-100 or beyond 100.

On dev, S4 computed it from the Phase 10 dev lists, which hold Dense's ranking to depth 100
(`data/phase14/fit.json`, `d8.dense_rank_split`: won 18 / 260 / 242, lost 31 / 19 / 47).

On `test-11`, S6 had only what S5 recorded.

## Problem

The S5 run records (`run-<system>.json`, `outcomes-<system>.jsonl.gz`) hold per-question metrics
(Full Support and gold recall at each budget, FS / GPR @k, `latency_ms`, and `sim_seconds` for
P14), **not the rankings**. Without Dense's `test-11` ranking, the Dense rank of a changed gold
paragraph cannot be read, so the 1-10 / 11-100 / beyond-100 split cannot be computed.

Retrieval cannot be re-run to recover the rankings: `test-11` is opened in a single pass (D6,
HU-3), `pass.json` refuses a second start, and re-retrieving held-out questions after seeing their
outcome is exactly what the protocol forbids, even descriptively.

## Root cause

S5 used Phase 10's run contract, which keeps metrics and not rankings: Phase 10 had no
ranking-based diagnostic on its held-out set. The Phase 14 plan (S5, S6) did not ask S5 to store
the rankings that D8's `test-11` half needs, and the gap was not noticed before the pass.

## Adopted solution

`cer p14-outcome` reports the split that the records allow, labelled as such in `outcome.json`
(`d8_test_11`, `computed_from_rankings: false`, with a note), derived from recorded values only:

- won and lost questions and their changed gold paragraphs are **exact** counts: in a won
  question P14's context holds every gold paragraph, and P10-C's gold recall @2,048 gives how
  many it missed (the reverse for a lost question);
- each changed paragraph's Dense rank is **bounded** by Dense's recorded gold recall @10: `1-10`
  when Dense's first 10 certainly hold it, `beyond-10` when they certainly do not (11-100 and
  beyond 100 cannot be separated), `undetermined` otherwise.

| P14 against P10-C, `test-11` | Questions | Changed gold paragraphs | Certainly Dense 1-10 | Certainly beyond 10 | Undetermined |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| won | 317 | 318 | 12 | 11 | 295 |
| lost | 72 | 72 | 20 | 5 | 47 |
| total | 389 | 390 | 32 | 16 | 342 |

Most changed paragraphs (342 of 390) are undetermined, so the bounded split says little about
where the held-out gain comes from. The dev split is the only full D8 split of this phase.

## Impact

- **Descriptive only.** D8 decides nothing (spec). The label `CANDIDATE_RELEVANCE_SUPPORTED`
  comes from D6 alone and is unaffected; D7 and D9 are unaffected.
- `14.results.md` reports the bounded split beside the dev split and lists this gap among its
  limitations.
- No artifact was changed; `test-11` was not retrieved on again.

## Prospective fix

A future pass stage whose spec asks for a ranking-based diagnostic on its held-out set stores the
rankings it needs (at least each component's ranking to the diagnostic's depth, per question) in
the same single pass, and its plan names that requirement in the pass step. No change is made to
the Phase 14 producer, which has run its one pass.
