## Problem and objective

**Problem.** Phase 10 measured P10-C (Dense + BM25 + the frozen Phase 9 Entity Hop, 0.5 / 0.3 /
0.2) at 66.50 % Full Support @2,048 on `test-10`, +3.92 pp over P10-B (Dense + BM25). The hop it
used is deliberately crude:

- it starts from **every** entity of P1, hubs included, which yields a median of about 5,300
  candidates per question and a latency tail of up to 16.5 s;
- it never reads the question.

A dev-only diagnostic run before this spec (`data/phase10/pre-spec-diagnostic/`, exploratory)
counted the 4,310 gold paragraphs Dense leaves outside its top 10 on the 7,405 dev questions:

- 2,651 share at least one entity with P1;
- 1,301 share one with an entity GLiNER reads in the question;
- **420 are reachable only from the question.**

Dropping frequent entities shrinks the candidate sets by an order of magnitude and loses some of
that reach. Reach is not ranking, so none of this is a result.

**Question.**

> Does a better use of the same entity signal — P1's entities without its hubs, entities read from
> the question, or both — retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than P10-C at FullWiki
> scale?

**Objective.** One candidate family, fitted on dev. The single configuration dev selects is
measured once on `test-11`:

- against **P10-C**, which decides the label, because it is the project's own bar for whether a new
  use of the entities is kept;
- against **P10-B**, reported with its own preregistered test, because Dense + BM25 is the
  reference the literature and the community understand.

This is **step 2 of 2** of the line Phase 10 opened. The author set the two comparisons on
2026-09-24.

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — The two new entity components

Both use the Phase 9 scoring unchanged: a candidate scores the sum of `log(1 + N / (1 + df))`
over the entity nodes it shares with the seed set. The rules below are also unchanged from
Phase 9:

- `read(q)`, the first 10 Dense units, is excluded;
- only positive scores are kept;
- ties break by unit id;
- lists are cut at depth 100;
- nothing is padded.

- **P1 hop, DF cap c.** The seed set is every entity node of P1 whose DF is at most c, where DF
  is the number of paragraphs of the Phase 9 index that hold the node (the `df` of the weight
  above). Rare and intermediate entities stay; only the hubs above c are dropped. With
  `c = all`, it is exactly the Phase 9 hop.
- **Question hop.** The seed set is the entity nodes whose normalized form equals a span GLiNER
  extracts from the question text. GLiNER runs with the Phase 9 model, revision, labels, threshold
  and normalization; nothing about the extractor changes. No title, gold or answer is read. A
  question whose spans match no node yields an empty list.

### D2 — The candidate family and the dev fit

P11 is `weighted` fusion with min-max normalization per component, the scheme P10-C used, over
four components in fixed order: Dense, BM25, the P1 hop at cap c and the question hop.

- **Grid:** `c ∈ {1,000, 3,000, 10,000, 30,000, all}`, crossed with every weight quadruple of multiples of 0.1 that
  sums to 1 (286 points). That is 1,430 configurations.
- **The grid contains the configurations each variant would have:**
  - "hubs dropped only": `w_question = 0`;
  - "P10-C + question": `c = all`;
  - P10-C itself: `c = all`, `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 / 0`.
- **Objective:** Full Support @2,048 over the 7,405 dev questions.
- **Ties** are broken by:
  1. gold recall @2,048;
  2. `w_question = 0` before `w_question > 0` (the query-time extractor must earn its place);
  3. the larger c (`all` is the largest), the one closest to the Phase 9 hop;
  4. the larger `w_dense`;
  5. the larger `w_bm25`;
  6. the larger `w_p1`.

### D3 — Laptop reproduction gate

Before the fit, the four-component code evaluated at the P10-C point (`c = all`,
`0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 / 0`) must reproduce P10-C's dev count **exactly: 4,801 / 7,405**, with 0
differing questions against the Phase 10 dev lists. A miss stops the phase and goes to the author.

### D4 — Dev gate

The selected configuration must reach **at least 4,838 / 7,405** dev Full Support @2,048, that is
P10-C's 4,801 plus 37 questions (0.5 pp). With about 1,400 configurations tried, the best dev
figure beats P10-C by some margin through selection alone; the margin keeps `test-11` from being
spent on that noise. Below it, the phase stops with `DEV_STOP` and `test-11` stays unopened.

### D5 — Primary endpoint and label

Full Support @2,048 on `test-11`, P11 against P10-C: one two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial on
the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
ENTITY_USE_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
ENTITY_USE_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
ENTITY_USE_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DEV_STOP                  D4
```

The label is written by code. Nothing else can change it.

### D6 — Secondary comparison, preregistered

P11 against P10-B on the same questions and metric, with the same exact McNemar test. It is
reported with wins, losses, delta and p, and it **cannot change the label**. The report also
gives P10-C against P10-B on `test-11`, which checks whether the Phase 10 gain repeats on fresh
questions (descriptive).

### D7 — Latency, reported, not gated

Each system's `retrieve` wall time per question is measured in the same pass on the laptop, as in
Phase 10: Dense scoring, BM25, the hops and fusion, excluding the BGE question encoding. Two
further rules:

- the GLiNER question extraction, if the selected configuration uses it, is **timed separately
  and reported beside** that figure, because it is new query-time work Phase 10 did not have;
- the report gives mean, median, p95, max and the ratios P11 / P10-C and P11 / P10-B.

## User stories

### HU-1 — Question entities, computed once

**As** the author, **I want** the question entities of dev and `test-11` extracted once and
frozen, **so that** the fit and the pass read the same spans.

- [ ] GLiNER spans for the 7,405 dev questions and the 5,000 `test-11` questions are written with
      the extractor's model, revision, labels, threshold and a digest per set.
- [ ] The `test-11` spans are only read by the held-out pass.
- [ ] BGE question vectors for `test-11` are encoded into their own cache (the
      `question_cache_key` gotcha).

### HU-2 — Reproduction, then the fit on dev

**As** the author, **I want** proof that the new code reproduces P10-C on dev before anything is
chosen, and the choice made on dev alone, **so that** the held-out comparison is honest.

- [ ] D3 reproduces 4,801 / 7,405 exactly, or the phase stops.
- [ ] The full 1,430-point curve is recorded, with the selected point and the tie rule applied.
- [ ] The dev gate is applied by code. `DEV_STOP` leaves `test-11` unopened.
- [ ] The selection is committed before the held-out pass.

### HU-3 — One held-out pass

**As** the author, **I want** P10-A, P10-B, P10-C and P11 measured once on `test-11`, **so that**
the answer is one number with its test.

- [ ] Runs only after the author's explicit authorization, and refuses a second start.
- [ ] Per-question outcomes are kept for the paired tests. The budget metrics are recorded at 512,
      1,024, 2,048 and 4,096 tokens, and FS/GPR at @2, @5, @10 and @20.
- [ ] Latency is recorded per D7.
- [ ] `outcome.json` records, for D5 and D6: wins, losses, ties, delta in points, exact p, the
      label and `stop_reasons`.

### HU-4 — Results

**As** the author, **I want** `11.results.md` with the answer against both bars, **so that** the
line has a measured position against its own best system and against the community reference.

- [ ] Every value is labelled measured, derived or projection.
- [ ] What the selected configuration changed is shown on dev: c, the question weight, the
      candidate-set sizes, and the gold paragraphs reached only from the question.
- [ ] `docs/research_summary.md` is updated only if the label is `ENTITY_USE_SUPPORTED` or
      `ENTITY_USE_REGRESSION`.

## Data contracts

- **`data/phase11/question-entities-<set>.json`** (dev, `test-11`): extractor pin, per-question
  spans and matched node ids, digest.
- **`data/phase11/reproduction.json`**: the P10-C dev count through the new code, against 4,801,
  and the differing qids.
- **`data/phase11/fit.json`**: the grid, the curve, the selected configuration, the tie rule, the
  dev gate verdict and P10-C's dev count. Committed before the pass.
- **`data/phase11/run-<system>.json`** and **`outcomes-<system>.jsonl.gz`**: the Phase 10 run
  contract, with provenance naming `test-11`, its vectors and each system's own weights.
- **`data/phase11/outcome.json`**: D5, D6, the label, `stop_reasons` and latency.

Every artifact is JSON or NPZ. No pickle. As in Phases 9 and 10, only `fit.json` is versioned in
git; the other artifacts are recorded by digest in `11.results.md`.

---

## Restrictions

- **R1.** No rented compute and no paid API. Everything runs on the laptop.
- **R2.** Phase 9 and Phase 10 artifacts are read-only. Phase 11 writes only under
  `data/phase11/` and its own question-vector cache.
- **R3.** Nothing is fitted or selected on `probe`, `test-10` or `test-11`. `test-10` is spent: it
  is neither retrieved on nor read again, not even descriptively.
- **R4.** P10-B and P10-C keep their frozen weights. The hop keeps its scoring, depth and
  exclusion rule.
- **R5.** The GLiNER extractor used on the questions is the Phase 9 one, pinned by revision and
  configuration digest.

---

## Anti-objectives

Phase 11 does **not**:

- re-extract the corpus, rebuild the entity index or change the normalization;
- canonicalize entities, link them to titles, use hyperlinks or entity types;
- add relations, a second hop, extra seeds beyond P1 and the question, or graph propagation;
- filter candidates by semantic relevance to the question (the roadmap's query-aware hop, 7.2);
- train anything, add a cross-encoder or put an LLM in retrieval;
- refit P10-B or P10-C, or try a second candidate family after a `DEV_STOP`.
