# Phase 15: the frozen systems on MuSiQue — implementation plan

> Functional spec: [`15.spec.md`](15.spec.md), approved 2026-09-29 and frozen. This plan decides
> HOW; it changes no spec rule. A change to any spec rule is a written deviation `15.Y_*.md`.
>
> Status (2026-09-29): **approved by the author**, with the five decisions recorded under
> "Author decisions" below. Nothing is implemented, downloaded or rented yet.
>
> **The author's condition on approval: reuse the code of the earlier phases**, the code already
> exercised on the FullWiki corpus. New code is limited to what the "Reuse" and "What the
> existing code does not yet support" notes list; an existing function is generalized with
> defaults that leave its earlier callers unchanged, never copied or rewritten.
>
> Simplification rule: one question, **eight steps**, no `15.tasks.md`, reuse before
> generalization. Code for a step past a stop state is not written (the Phase 12-14 precedent).
>
> Where things run: a **rented RunPod GPU** for the passage and question encoding and the GLiNER
> extraction only (R1); the **laptop** for the download, the corpus, the gold mapping, token
> counts, BM25, the entity index, the integrity checks, the pass, the outcome and the refit.
> Projected rented cost: **about 1 USD, under 2 USD** (projection; basis in the technical notes).

## Objective

> With every weight and parameter frozen at its HotpotQA value, do the Entity Hop systems
> (P10-C and P14) still retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than Dense + BM25 on
> MuSiQue?

One pass, nothing fitted: P10-A (Dense), P10-B (Dense + BM25, 0.5 / 0.5), P10-C (Dense + BM25 +
Entity Hop, 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2) and P14 (Dense + BM25 + relevance-ordered hop, `α = 0.75`,
0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2) are measured once on the 2,417 validation questions of MuSiQue-Ans, over a
corpus pooled from every MuSiQue-Ans train and validation paragraph.

Primary endpoint (D5): Full Support @2,048 on the 2,417 validation questions, **P14 against
P10-B**, one two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial on the discordant questions, p = 1/2),
α = 0.05. Terminal states, written by code:

```text
TRANSFER_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
TRANSFER_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DATA_STOP               D2 or D4
```

Preregistered secondaries (D6), same metric and test, none can change the label: P14 against
P10-C, P10-C against P10-B, P10-B against P10-A. Reported, deciding nothing: the ladder beside
HotpotQA `test-11` (D6), the split by number of supporting paragraphs (D7), what the hop reaches
(D8), the refitted ceiling (D9, exploratory), latency (D10).

## Author stop points

1. **Before renting the GPU (end of S3's code, before its pod half).** The recipe
   `15.runpod_recipe.md`, the contracted hourly rate, the frozen commit and the upload / download
   checklist are presented. No money is spent before the author says go. On the pod, if the
   session reaches **2 USD**, the pod is stopped and the author is asked before anything else.
2. **Any `DATA_STOP` or integrity miss.** `DATA_STOP` from the gold mapping (S2); a source whose
   bytes, SHA-256 or row counts differ from the pins (S2); a GLiNER configuration digest on the
   pod that is not the Phase 9 pod digest (S3, checked before a paragraph is read); more than
   1 % of the corpus units longer than GLiNER's window (S3, counted on the laptop before the pod
   is rented); a D4 miss (S4): the HotpotQA code-identity counts, the extractor identity or the
   live path.
3. **Before the single pass on the validation questions (S5).** It runs once, after
   `integrity.json` is committed, only with `--authorized-pass` and the author's explicit word.
4. Two attempts without progress.

    `P10_TRAIN_SPLIT` lives in `cli.py`.
  - New `corpus/musique.py`:
    - the source pin (see technical notes): the two file URLs at revision
      `22873a405dd809893b22ada0b499299fb612d2df`, their bytes and SHA-256, and the license line;
    - `unit_of(paragraph)`: `IndexingUnit(unit_id_for(title, (paragraph_text,)), title,

    - `write_corpus` / `write_questions` (write-once, JSON lines gzip with `mtime=0`), with a

      and refuses on `DATA_STOP` or another corpus.
  - → `config.py`, `corpus/musique.py` (new).
  - Test `tests/corpus/test_musique.py` (written first, seen red):
    - one paragraph attached to two questions gives one unit; a paragraph supporting for one
      question and a distractor for another is one unit, gold only for the first;
    - two paragraphs with the same title and different text are two units;
    - gold keeps 3 and 4 paragraphs; a question whose supporting paragraphs collapse is recorded;
    - the mapping digest changes when any gold id changes, the third and fourth included;
    - the `DATA_STOP` share at exactly 1 % (goes) and just past it (stops);
    - `fullwiki.load_corpus` reads the written corpus and refuses an edited line.

### Gold after deduplication

A unit is `unit_id_for(title, (paragraph_text,))`: the Phase 1 content id, a hash of title and
text, so deduplication by title and text is the id itself. Gold is per question, never per unit:
a question's gold ids are the ids of its own `is_supporting` paragraphs. The same paragraph can
be gold for one question and a distractor for another; the corpus holds it once and only the
first question counts it as gold. Two questions with the same supporting paragraph share the
unit. Because the gold paragraphs are in the pool by construction, unmapped gold is expected to
be 0: the D2 rule (more than 1 % of questions with an unmapped gold, the Phase 10 convention of
a share of questions) is a guard, not an expected stop. Text is taken as written, with no
whitespace or Unicode normalization; two copies that differ only in whitespace stay two units
(declared, counted).

### Full Support with 3 or 4 gold paragraphs (verified in code)

- `evaluation/metrics.py`: `full_support(context, gold)` is `set(gold) <= set(context)` and
  `gold_recall` divides by `len(set(gold))`; `recall_at_k` uses `gold_recall`. Any number of gold
  paragraphs works.
- `corpus/pool.py`: `Question.gold_unit_ids` is `tuple[str, ...]`.
- `evaluation/harness.py` `evaluate_retriever` and `evaluation/fullwiki.py` `measure_system`
  pass `question.gold_unit_ids` through unchanged; `phase14.dense_rank_split` and
  `coverage_changes` iterate over the whole gold set.
- **Not n-gold ready (verified):** `fullwiki._mapping_digest` writes `gold_unit_ids[0]` and
  `[1]` only; `fullwiki.load_questions`, `fullwiki.build_questions` / `supporting_titles`,
  `phase10._set_questions` and `phase10._read_set` build exactly two gold ids; `pool._resolve_gold`
  requires two. Phase 15 therefore writes and reads its questions with `corpus/musique.py`, never
  with these.
- By construction, FS @2 is 0 for every 3- and 4-gold question (1,165 questions, derived); it is
  reported as is and D7's split makes it visible.

### The hop's `N` and `df`

`second_hop.node_weights(index)` is `rarity_weights(concept_support(index.incidence),
index.incidence.shape[0])`: `N` is the number of rows of the index handed in and `df` each
node's column support in it. Built by `phase9.build_entity_index` over the Phase 15 records and
unit ids, the index gives the new corpus's `N` and `df` with no code change (spec, "Hop" row).

### What the existing code does not yet support (verified)

- `_phase_9_inputs` and `cmd_fullwiki_build --stage embed` read Phase 9's question format
  (two gold) and directories: Phase 15 adds `_p15_inputs` in the same shape.
- `_fullwiki_extract` fixes the model directory (`data/phase9/models/gliner`), the output
  directory (`gliner/`) and `"phase": 9`: generalized with defaults.
- `phase9.write_run` stores metrics, not rankings (deviation 14.1): `phase15.write_rankings`.
- `phase11.paired` refuses any run that is not 5,000 `test-11` questions: `phase15.paired`.
- `_p14_peak_memory_mb` returns the peak working set only; D10 asks for the working set before
  each system: a small reader of the current `WorkingSetSize` (same `GetProcessMemoryInfo` call).
- `download.ensure_corpus` checks SHA-256 only: the stage checks bytes too.
- `phase9.build_embedding` labels the question cache key with `standard-dev`: cosmetic, the cache
  directory is Phase 15's own.
- MuSiQue paragraphs are not sentence-split. `local_extraction.windows_for` splits a paragraph
  over GLiNER's 384-token window at its sentence boundaries; a Phase 15 unit has one segment, so
  an over-long paragraph is read in one window the model truncates. The build records how many
  units exceed the window (measured then). Open question 3.

### Author decisions (2026-09-29, before implementation)

1. **Source.** The Hugging Face copy's release files at the pinned revision, held to the bytes
   and SHA-256 above, with no comparison against the official Google Drive zip. That the two are
   byte-identical is not verified; `15.results.md` declares it.
2. **D9's hop lists.** Re-fusing over the 330 points needs the hop at `α = 0.25, 0.5, 1`, which
   no frozen system produces. They are stored in the single pass with an untimed `hop_all` over
   the stored Dense lists (S5), checked against the live `α = 0` and `α = 0.75` lists. No metric
   is computed from them before the label.
3. **Long paragraphs and GLiNER's window.** No sentence splitter is added. The units longer than
   the window are counted and declared; above 1 % of the corpus the phase stops and goes to the
   author (S2, stop point 2).
4. **D7.** All four comparisons of D5 and D6 are reported in each 2 / 3 / 4 group, all
   descriptive. The spec's "three" was a drafting slip.
5. **Near-duplicate gold.** Found in review: MuSiQue sometimes carries two nearly equal versions
   of a passage, only one marked supporting. The spec's rule stands (gold is the marked
   paragraph); S2 counts the near-duplicates over the pooled corpus and `15.results.md` declares
   the effect: every system's absolute figures are slightly understated, the paired comparisons
   are not biased towards any system.
