# Spec Phase 15: an untrained second-hop query, "question + P1", as a fourth component

> Functional spec: WHAT is measured and WHY. The HOW goes in the plan (`15.0_*.md`).
> Status: **draft, awaiting the author's approval. Not frozen.** The direction (the untrained
> second-hop query reformulation) was decided by the author on 2026-09-29
> ([`review_2026-09-29.md`](../review_2026-09-29.md), §8) and is not reopened here. The four open
> decisions below (held-out set, text form, fourth component, exclusion rule) were settled by the
> author on 2026-09-29, before any Phase 15 measurement. **The spec cannot be frozen until Phase
> 14's label exists** (`data/phase14/outcome.json`, written by code): the label fixes the
> reference system (D1). Once approved, a change to any rule below is a written deviation
> `15.Y_*.md`.

---

## Problem and objective

**Problem.** Every structural lever tried since Phase 9 starts from the entities P1 shares with
other paragraphs. The review of 2026-09-29 (dev, gold-informed, exploratory) found that 1,063 of
P10-C's 2,604 dev failures are **reach** failures: a gold paragraph is in none of the three
component lists (Dense top 100, BM25 top 100, the hop list), so no reordering of those lists can
support them. It also found that what reaches the second gold paragraph is usually that the
paragraph is *about* something P1 names, which a shared raw entity form expresses poorly.

A second Dense search whose query is the question **together with the text of P1** needs nothing
from the corpus: no entities, titles or links. It is the version without a concept space of the
project's original iterative-retrieval hypothesis, and the untrained form of the second-hop query
MDR trains (Xiong et al. 2021). The literature scan of 2026-09-29 found no paper reporting the
untrained form with a dense encoder on FullWiki.

**Question.**

> Does adding, as a fourth fused component, a Dense search whose query is the BGE vector of
> "question + text of P1" retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than the line's best
> system at FullWiki scale?

**Objective.** One candidate family, fitted on dev. The single configuration dev selects is
measured once on `test-15`:

- against the **reference system** (D1), which decides the label;
- against **P10-B** (Dense + BM25), with its own preregistered test, because it is the reference
  the community understands. It cannot change the label.

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — The reference system

The reference is the line's best system when this spec is frozen, decided by Phase 14's label:

| Phase 14 label | Reference | Its hop, used inside P15 | Reference on dev | Dev bar (D7) |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| `CANDIDATE_RELEVANCE_SUPPORTED` | **P14** | the relevance-ordered hop at `α = 0.75` | 5,224 / 7,405 | 5,257 |
| any other label | **P10-C** | the Phase 9 hop | 4,801 / 7,405 | 4,834 |

The dev counts are measured (Phase 14 `fit.json`, Phase 10 `fit.json`); the bars are derived
(D7). The row that applies is written into this spec when it is frozen; the other is deleted.

### D2 — The reformulated query

- **Text.** `question + " " + indexable_text(P1)`: the question, one space, then P1's
  `indexable_text` (`f"{title}. {plaintext}"`), the same text Dense encoded for that paragraph.
  P1 is Dense's first unit for the question, as in the hop.
- **Order.** The question comes first, so that any truncation removes the end of P1, never the
  question.
- **Query prompt: the one Dense uses for questions, which is empty.** The Phase 9 encoding
  recorded `query_prompt: ""` for `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`; the project has never prefixed a
  BGE-small question. The reformulated query is encoded under the same (empty) prompt, so its
  vector lives in the space Dense's question vectors live in.
- **Truncation.** The model's resolved `max_seq_length` applies and is recorded. The share of
  texts truncated is recorded, counted with the model's own tokenizer.
- **One form.** No second form is tried. No separator, prompt, order or P1 text variant enters
  the grid.
- **One seed, one round.** Only P1. The reformulated search's own results are never fed back.
- **Encoder.** The pinned BGE-small backend of Phase 9, on CPU, no training of any kind.

### D3 — The reformulated component

- **Score.** `v_r · v_c`, the dot product of the reformulated vector with every row of the
  Phase 9 passage cache (brute force, exact), in float32.
- **Exclusion.** `read(q)`, Dense's first 10 units, is excluded, as in the hop. P1 is in
  `read(q)`.
- **List.** The remaining units ranked by score descending, then unit id ascending, cut at depth
  100. No padding. Each unit carries its dot product as the score the fusion reads.

### D4 — The held-out set, a new partition

`test-10` and `test-11` are spent (`test-11` by the Phase 14 pass started 2026-09-29). Of the
15,661 `hard` questions in the pinned HotpotQA train release, 11,000 were drawn in Phase 10;
**4,661 remain** (derived).

- **Source.** The Phase 10 train release, held to its recorded SHA-256
  (`8512e677…61c9`), and `data/phase10/questions.json`, read-only.
- **Draw.** The remaining `hard` qids are those in none of `probe`, `test-10` and `test-11`.
  `random.Random(15).sample(sorted remaining qids, 4,661)` is sliced in set order:

| Set | Size | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| `test-15` | 2,330 | Phase 15 held-out |
| `test-16` | 2,331 | reserve for a later phase; frozen by qid and digest, never retrieved on in Phase 15 |

- **Checks, or `DATA_STOP`.** The remaining count is 4,661; the two sets are disjoint from each
  other, from `probe`, `test-10`, `test-11` and from the 7,405 validation qids; gold titles are
  resolved under the Phase 9 D4 rule, unresolved gold stays in the denominator as a miss, and
  more than 1 % unresolved in either set stops the phase.
- **These are the last fresh `hard` train questions.** After `test-16` no further held-out set
  of this kind exists (limitation 3).

### D5 — The candidate family and the dev fit

P15 is `weighted` fusion with min-max normalization per component, the scheme of P10-C, P11 and
P14, over four components in fixed order: Dense, BM25, the reference hop, the reformulated
component.

- **Grid:** the 286 weight quadruples of multiples of 0.1 that sum to 1, Phase 11's grid.
- **The grid contains the reference exactly:** `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 / 0`.
- **Objective:** Full Support @2,048 over the 7,405 dev questions.
- **Ties** are broken by:
  1. gold recall @2,048;
  2. `w_reformulated = 0` before `w_reformulated > 0` (the new component must earn its place);
  3. the larger `w_dense`;
  4. the larger `w_bm25`;
  5. the larger `w_hop`.

### D6 — Laptop reproduction gate

Before the fit, the four-component code at `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 / 0` must reproduce the reference's
dev count **exactly** (5,224 or 4,801 of 7,405, per D1), with 0 differing questions against the
reference rebuilt from its recorded dev lists. A zero-weighted component still adds its units to
the fused union; the gate measures that this changes nothing, it is not assumed. A miss stops the
phase and goes to the author.

### D7 — Dev gate

The selected configuration must exceed the reference's dev count by **at least 33 questions**.
The margin rescales Phase 11's 37 questions for 1,430 configurations to this grid, as in Phases
12-14:

`37 × √(ln 286 / ln 1,430) = 32.65`, rounded up to 33 (derived).

Below the bar, or if the fit selects `w_reformulated = 0`, the phase stops with `DEV_STOP` and
`test-15` stays unopened.

### D8 — Contamination probe, before `test-15` is opened

BGE-small was fine-tuned on HotpotQA train, and `test-15` comes from train. Phase 10 bounded the
effect on Dense (+2.89 pp on `probe` against dev, within its 5-point margin). The reformulated
vector is a new use of the same encoder, so it gets its own probe, on the **existing** 1,000
`probe` questions (their only use; never a result; no new question is spent).

- **Measure.** Over the gold paragraphs outside `read(q)`, the share the reformulated component
  lists within its depth 100, on `probe` and on dev.
- **Rule.** `probe share − dev share > 5.0 percentage points` → `DATA_STOP`. Otherwise the phase
  continues and the difference is reported.
- It runs only if the dev gate passes, and before the author is asked to authorize the pass.

### D9 — Primary endpoint and label

Full Support @2,048 on `test-15`, P15 against the reference: one two-sided exact McNemar test
(binomial on the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
QUERY_REFORMULATION_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
QUERY_REFORMULATION_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
QUERY_REFORMULATION_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DEV_STOP                           D7
DATA_STOP                          D4 or D8
```

The label is written by code. Nothing else can change it. `test-15` is opened in a single pass,
only after the author authorizes it explicitly, just before it runs.

### D10 — Secondary comparison, preregistered

P15 against P10-B on the same questions and metric, with the same exact McNemar test, α = 0.05.
It is reported with wins, losses, delta and p, and it **cannot change the label**. The report
also gives the reference against P10-B and, if the reference is P14, P14 against P10-C, on
`test-15` (descriptive).

### D11 — Where the gains come from, reported, deciding nothing

The reformulated query uses Dense's own encoder, so part of a gain may re-weight what Dense
already ranks. Reported descriptively, as Phase 14's D8:

- **on dev**, for the selected configuration against the reference, and **on `test-15`** for
  P15 against the reference: for every question won and every question lost, each gold
  paragraph whose coverage within the 2,048-token budget changed, classified by its Dense rank:
  1-10, 11-100, or beyond 100;
- **the component's own reach on dev:** of the 4,310 gold paragraphs outside Dense's top 10, how
  many the reformulated list holds in its first 5 and in its depth 100, split by whether P1 is
  gold, and how many of those are in none of the reference's component lists.

### D12 — Latency, reported, not gated

Each system's `retrieve` wall time per question is measured in the same pass on the laptop, as
in Phases 10-14, excluding the BGE encoding of the question. For P15 two pieces of new
query-time work are **timed separately** and reported beside `retrieve` and summed with it:

- the extra BGE encode of the reformulated text, per question;
- the second brute-force Dense search.

The report gives mean, median, p95, max and the ratios P15 / reference and P15 / P10-B. No
threshold.

## User stories

### HU-1 — Frozen question sets

**As** the author, **I want** `test-15` and `test-16` drawn, gold-mapped and frozen before any
retrieval on them, **so that** no choice can depend on them.

- [ ] The D4 draw and checks are recorded, each set with a qid digest and a mapping digest.
- [ ] Plain question vectors for `test-15` go to a cache of Phase 15's own, under a key that
      names the set. `test-16` is not encoded.

### HU-2 — The reformulated lists on dev

**As** the author, **I want** the reformulated list of every dev question built once from fixed
inputs, **so that** the fit reads fixed lists.

- [ ] P1, `read(q)` and the Dense, BM25 and hop lists come from the recorded dev lists of
      Phases 10 and 14, digest-checked, not re-run.
- [ ] The reformulated vectors are written to **Phase 15's own cache**, under a key that names
      the model, revision, the set, the text form and the digest of the Dense lists that fixed
      P1. `question_cache_key` includes neither the corpus nor P1's text, so it is not used as
      it is.
- [ ] The Phase 9 passage vectors are verified against the digest `embedding.json` records.
- [ ] Encoding seconds, search seconds, truncated share, peak memory and host are recorded.

### HU-3 — Reproduction, then the fit on dev

**As** the author, **I want** proof that the new code reproduces the reference on dev before
anything is chosen, and the choice made on dev alone, **so that** the held-out comparison is
honest.

- [ ] D6 reproduces the reference's dev count exactly, or the phase stops.
- [ ] The full 286-point curve is recorded, with the selected point and the tie rule applied.
- [ ] The dev gate is applied by code. `DEV_STOP` leaves `test-15` unopened.
- [ ] The D11 dev figures are recorded with the fit.
- [ ] The selection is committed before the held-out pass.

### HU-4 — Probe, then one held-out pass

**As** the author, **I want** the contamination probe recorded and then every system measured
once on `test-15`, **so that** the answer is one number with its test.

- [ ] D8 is computed and recorded, with its verdict, before `test-15` is loaded.
- [ ] The pass runs only after the author's explicit authorization, and refuses a second start.
- [ ] It measures P10-A, P10-B, P10-C, P14 (if it is the reference) and P15.
- [ ] The `test-15` reformulated vectors are encoded inside the pass, since P1 on `test-15` is
      itself a retrieval result.
- [ ] Per-question outcomes are kept. The budget metrics are recorded at 512, 1,024, 2,048 and
      4,096 tokens, and FS/GPR at @2, @5, @10 and @20.
- [ ] `outcome.json` records, for D9 and D10: wins, losses, ties, delta in points, exact p, the
      label and `stop_reasons`; and the D11 split and D12 latency.

### HU-5 — Results

**As** the author, **I want** `15.results.md` with the answer at each step reached, **so that**
the line knows whether the reformulated query is worth keeping.

- [ ] Every value is labelled measured, derived, projection or interpretation.
- [ ] Every result states both comparisons: against the reference and against P10-B.
- [ ] `docs/research_summary.md` is updated only if the label is
      `QUERY_REFORMULATION_SUPPORTED` or `QUERY_REFORMULATION_REGRESSION`.

## Restrictions

- **R1.** No rented compute and no paid API. Everything runs on the laptop.
- **R2.** Phase 9-14 artifacts, dev lists and caches are read-only. Phase 15 writes only under
  `data/phase15/`.
- **R3.** Nothing is fitted or selected on `probe`, `test-15` or `test-16`. `test-10` and
  `test-11` are spent: they are neither retrieved on nor read again, not even descriptively.
- **R4.** Every reference system keeps its frozen configuration.
- **R5.** The reformulated query uses the Phase 9 Dense model, revision and passage vectors.
- **R6.** Nothing of the reformulated component is measured on dev before this spec is approved.

---

## Anti-objectives

Phase 15 does **not**:

- train or fine-tune anything;
- put an LLM or a cross-encoder in the loop;
- use more than one seed, or a second round of reformulation;
- try a second text form, prompt, separator or exclusion rule;
- change the hop, refit `α`, or refit any reference system;
- use titles as structure, hyperlinks, entity linking or canonicalization;
- change the encoder or re-embed the corpus;
- retrieve on `test-16`, or try a second candidate family after a `DEV_STOP`.

## Limitations, declared in advance

1. **Dev has been fitted by Phases 10-14** and read with gold-informed ceilings. It remains
   valid for selection, not as held-out evidence.
2. **A smaller held-out set.** `test-15` has 2,330 questions against the 5,000 of `test-10` and
   `test-11`, so the test detects smaller differences less reliably. As a projection, with about
   10 % of questions discordant the smallest difference it can call significant is near 1.3
   points.
3. **The supply of fresh questions ends with `test-16`.** A later held-out set must come from
   another level of HotpotQA train, which does not match dev's level, or from another benchmark
   (roadmap 7.1).
4. **Train questions, a fine-tuned encoder.** D8 bounds the effect on the new component; it
   cannot remove it. The references share the bias through their Dense component.
5. **A wrong P1 is inherited.** P1 is not gold for 22.6 % of dev questions (measured, review of
   2026-09-29). The reformulated query then describes the wrong paragraph.
6. **The vector is mostly P1.** P1's text is several times longer than the question, so the
   search may return paragraphs that resemble P1 rather than paragraphs that answer the
   question. D11 reports what it reaches; one form means this is not tuned.
7. **The same encoder twice.** A gain may partly re-weight what Dense already ranks. D11 reports
   how much, descriptively, and cannot separate the two causally.
8. **An encoder used outside its training shape.** BGE-small was trained on short queries
   against passages; a query that contains a passage is a use it was not trained for.
