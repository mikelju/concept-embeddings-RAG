# Spec Phase 15: the frozen systems on MuSiQue

> Functional spec: WHAT is measured and WHY. The HOW goes in the plan (`15.0_*.md`).
> Status: **approved by the author on 2026-09-29 and frozen.** Settled by the author on
> 2026-09-29, before any Phase 15 measurement: validation on another corpus comes before any new
> component; MuSiQue is the first corpus; the questions are its 2,417 validation questions; the
> corpus pools every MuSiQue paragraph (D1); the label is decided against Dense + BM25 (D5), with
> P10-C and P14 both measured to show the progression (D6); the refitted ceiling is kept as an
> exploratory figure (D9); entities are extracted on a rented RunPod GPU, within the projected
> cost. A change to any rule below is a written deviation `15.Y_*.md`.

---

## Problem and objective

**Problem.** Every held-out result of this line was measured on HotpotQA. The review of
2026-09-29 measured that 79.9 % of HotpotQA's dev gold pairs are joined by a first-paragraph
hyperlink, which is how its bridge questions were built. P10-C's and P14's gains over
Dense + BM25 may therefore depend on that construction. Their weights and P14's `α` were fitted
on HotpotQA dev with gold labels, which a user with a private corpus does not have.

MuSiQue (Trivedi et al. 2022) is Wikipedia too, but its questions were composed from single-hop
questions and filtered so that they cannot be answered by a shortcut, and they need 2, 3 or 4
paragraphs. BGE-small was not fine-tuned on it.

**Question.**

> With every weight and parameter frozen at its HotpotQA value, do the Entity Hop systems
> (P10-C and P14) still retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than Dense + BM25 on
> MuSiQue?

**Objective.** One pass, nothing fitted. Four frozen systems are measured once on the 2,417
validation questions of MuSiQue-Ans, over a corpus pooled from MuSiQue's own paragraphs.

This phase adds no retriever and changes none.

## Decisions

### D1 — The corpus

- **Source.** MuSiQue-Ans, train and validation, pinned by URL, bytes and SHA-256 at download.
  Examined on 2026-09-29 through the Hugging Face copy `bdsaglam/musique` (revision
  `22873a40…12df`), configuration `answerable`: 19,938 train and 2,417 validation questions
  (measured). The plan pins the source it downloads from and checks these counts.
- **Pool.** The corpus is every paragraph attached to any train or validation question
  of MuSiQue-Ans, deduplicated by title and text. The validation questions alone carry 21,100
  unique paragraphs (measured); the literature reports about 84,000 for the pool of all splits
  (IRCoT, Trivedi et al. 2023; not verified here, measured by the fetch stage). Pooling the
  train paragraphs makes the corpus larger and the search harder, and uses no train question.
- **Indexing unit.** One MuSiQue paragraph, whole, with `indexable_text = f"{title}. {text}"`
  as in Phase 9. No fixed-size window. MuSiQue's paragraphs are passages of Wikipedia articles
  (median 72 words, maximum 299, measured on validation), not always an article's own
  paragraph; that is declared, not changed.
- **Size.** Tens of thousands of paragraphs against FullWiki's 5,233,329. This phase tests
  another construction of questions, not scale (limitation 2).

### D2 — The questions and the gold

- The 2,417 validation questions, all answerable: 1,252 with 2 supporting paragraphs, 760 with
  3 and 405 with 4 (measured).
- **Gold** is the set of paragraphs MuSiQue marks `is_supporting` for the question, mapped to
  corpus units by title and text. Any unmapped gold stays in the denominator as a miss; any
  unmapped gold at all is reported, and more than 1 % stops the phase (`DATA_STOP`).
- **Full Support** means every supporting paragraph of the question is inside the 2,048-token
  context: 2, 3 or 4 of them.
- The train questions are not read for retrieval, fitting or diagnosis. They stay a reserve for
  later phases.

### D3 — Nothing is fitted

No weight, `α`, depth, threshold or prompt is chosen on MuSiQue. The four systems run with the
values in the table above. There is no dev set in this phase and no grid.

### D4 — Integrity before the pass

- **Code identity.** On HotpotQA dev, from the recorded dev lists, the code used in this phase
  reproduces P10-C at 4,801 / 7,405 and P14 at 5,224 / 7,405 exactly, before it touches
  MuSiQue. A miss stops the phase.
- **Extractor identity.** The GLiNER configuration digest on the pod equals the Phase 9 pod
  digest. A different digest stops the phase and goes to the author.
- **Live path.** The pass runs the live retrievers; before it, they run on the first 200
  questions of MuSiQue **train** sorted by id, only to check that they complete and that the
  run and ranking records are well formed. No metric of those 200 is computed, printed or
  stored.

### D5 — Primary endpoint and label

Full Support @2,048 on the 2,417 validation questions, **P14 against P10-B**: one two-sided exact
McNemar test (binomial on the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
TRANSFER_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
TRANSFER_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DATA_STOP               D2 or D4
```

P10-B is the primary control here because the question is whether the structural signal is
worth having at all outside HotpotQA. The label is written by code. The pass runs once, after
the author's explicit authorization, and refuses a second start.

### D6 — Secondary comparisons, preregistered

Same metric and test. None can change the label.

- **P14 against P10-C:** does ordering the hop's candidates by the question still help?
- **P10-C against P10-B:** does the query-blind hop still help?
- **P10-B against P10-A:** the reference gain of BM25, for scale.

Every result states both comparisons of the line: against P10-C and against P10-B.

**The progression, side by side.** The author asked whether each step of the line adds on
MuSiQue as it did on HotpotQA. The report gives the ladder Dense → Dense + BM25 → P10-C → P14
as Full Support @2,048 and as the gain of each step over the one before, on MuSiQue validation
beside the same four systems on HotpotQA `test-11` (Phase 14, measured: 57.04 %, 61.58 %,
65.70 %, 70.60 %). The two columns differ in corpus size and in questions, so they compare the
direction and order of the gains, not their size.

### D7 — By number of hops, reported, deciding nothing

Every system's Full Support and gold recall @2,048, and the paired comparisons (all four: corrected
2026-09-29, author decision 4 in the plan; "three" was a drafting slip), split by
questions of 2, 3 and 4 supporting paragraphs. The hop makes one step from one seed, so its gain
is expected to shrink as the hops grow (hypothesis, not a measurement).

### D8 — What the hop reaches, reported, deciding nothing

On the validation questions, from the rankings the pass stores (the prospective fix of
deviation 14.1): for each system, how many supporting paragraphs outside Dense's first 10 each
component lists; whether P1 is a supporting paragraph; the hop's candidate-set sizes; and the
split of won and lost questions by the Dense rank of each changed paragraph (1-10, 11-100,
beyond 100).

### D9 — Weights refitted on MuSiQue, an optimistic ceiling

After the pass and the label, the stored component rankings are re-fused over Phase 14's
330-point grid (5 `α` × 66 weight triples) and the best point is reported beside the frozen
one. It is fitted and measured on the same questions, so it is an **upper bound, exploratory**:
it shows how much is lost by not being able to fit, and it is never reported as a result of a
system.

### D10 — Latency, reported, not gated

`retrieve` wall time per question on the laptop, as in Phases 10-14, with the order in which the
systems ran and the working set before each. The corpus is about 60 times smaller than
FullWiki, so the figures do not compare with earlier phases.

- [ ] D4 passes, or the phase stops.
- [ ] The pass runs only after the author's explicit authorization and refuses a second start.

      component's ranking and the fused ranking to depth 100**.

      interpretation, and puts the HotpotQA figures of the same systems beside the MuSiQue

      is a result of the line.

## Anti-objectives

Phase 15 does **not**:

- add or change a retriever, a component, a weight or `α`;
- add hops or seeds for the 3- and 4-hop questions;
- use MuSiQue's question decomposition, answers or train questions;
- change the encoder, the extractor or the indexing unit;
- run the query reformulation (Phase 17) or the non-Wikipedia corpus (Phase 16);
- report the refitted ceiling (D9) as a system's result;
- compare its absolute figures with published MuSiQue figures without stating the differences
  in corpus and metric.

---

## Limitations, declared in advance

1. **Still Wikipedia.** MuSiQue changes how the questions were built, not the kind of text.
   Phase 16 is the test on another kind of text.
2. **A small corpus.** With tens of thousands of paragraphs, Dense alone reaches further than
   at FullWiki scale and the hop's candidate sets are smaller. A gain or its absence here says
   nothing about scale.
3. **Each question brings its own distractors.** MuSiQue attaches up to 20 paragraphs to each
   question, chosen to be confusable with the gold. In the pooled corpus they are hard
   negatives for their own question and ordinary paragraphs for the others.
4. **One hop against 2-4 hop questions.** The systems were designed for two-paragraph
   evidence. D7 reports the split; a loss on 3- and 4-hop questions is a limit of the design.
5. **A frozen transfer is a lower bound on what fitting could give**, and D9's ceiling an upper
   bound. The honest figure for a corpus without labels is the frozen one.
6. **One benchmark more, not generality.** Two Wikipedia benchmarks agreeing is evidence, not
   proof, that the mechanism is general.
