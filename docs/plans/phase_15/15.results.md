Every value is labelled: **measured** (read from an artifact of this phase or of the phase named),
**derived** (arithmetic on measured values, with the arithmetic shown where it is not a plain
difference), **exploratory** (descriptive or fitted on the same questions it is measured on;
decides nothing), **interpretation** (a reading, not a measurement) or **projection**.
Percentages are rounded half up from the exact counts, to two decimals.

## Result

**Terminal state: `TRANSFER_SUPPORTED`.**

Source: `data/phase15/outcome.json`, written mechanically by `cer p15-outcome` (code `8d79510`) from
the four run files; `stop_reasons` is empty.

Primary endpoint (D5): Full Support @2,048 tokens on the 2,417 validation questions of MuSiQue-Ans,
**P14 against P10-B**, every weight and `α` frozen at its HotpotQA value.

| System | Full Support @2,048 | Share | Label |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P10-B Dense + BM25 (0.5 / 0.5, frozen) | 524 / 2,417 | 21.68 % | measured |
| P14 Dense + BM25 + relevance-ordered hop (`α = 0.75`, 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2, frozen) | **761 / 2,417** | **31.49 %** | measured |
| Difference | +237 | **+9.81 pp** | derived (9.81 recorded) |

```text
exact two-sided McNemar: wins 294, losses 57, ties 2,066   p = 1.3e-39   (alpha = 0.05)
```

Both comparisons of the line, at the primary budget (measured, `outcome.json`):

- **against P10-B** (the community reference): P14 +9.81 pp (294 wins / 57 losses), P10-C +6.00 pp
  (189 / 44);
- **against P10-C** (the project's bar before P14): P14 +3.81 pp (151 wins / 59 losses,
  p = 1.7e-10).

The answer to the phase question: **yes.** With nothing refitted, both Entity Hop systems retrieve
the full multi-hop evidence more often than Dense + BM25 on MuSiQue, and P14 more often than P10-C.
The ladder Dense → Dense + BM25 → P10-C → P14 rises at every step, as on HotpotQA `test-11`. The
gain comes almost entirely from the 2-paragraph questions; on the 4-paragraph questions no system
retrieves the full evidence (D7, below).

What D7 shows, plainly (derived from the tables):

- **The gain lives in the 2-paragraph questions.** Of P14's net +237 questions over P10-B, +211
  come from the 2-paragraph group, +26 from the 3-paragraph group and 0 from the 4-paragraph group
  (89.0 % from 2-paragraph questions: 211 / 237).
- **On 3-paragraph questions the gains are small.** P14 still beats both controls (p = 0.0022 and
  0.032, descriptive), but P10-C's gain over P10-B is not significant (p = 0.13), and the hop
  systems' mean gold recall is within 0.79 points of P10-B's (56.62 and 56.14 against 55.83).
  Interpretation: the hop adds little evidence here and mostly completes a few sets.
- **On 4-paragraph questions no system retrieves the full evidence.** At most 2 of 405 questions
  are fully supported by any system. Both hop systems have a **lower** mean gold recall than
  Dense + BM25 (P10-C −0.87 points, P14 −1.11 points). Interpretation: at 2,048 tokens the hop's
  candidates take context space that BM25's hits held. The differences are small and untested for
  recall.
- This matches the spec's expectation that a one-step hop from one seed helps less as the hops
  grow (limitation 4; the expectation was a hypothesis, this is its measurement). Whether the
  4-paragraph failure is about the hop or the budget (four MuSiQue paragraphs are about 407 tokens,
  derived from 101.8 per unit, well inside 2,048, so the budget is not the binding constraint by
  size alone) is not measured (interpretation).

## Limitations

Declared in the spec in advance (1-6), then found in the run (7-12).

1. **Still Wikipedia.** MuSiQue changes how the questions were built, not the kind of text.
   Phase 16 is the test on another kind of text.
2. **A small corpus.** 101,962 paragraphs, 51.3 times fewer than FullWiki; the hop's candidate sets
   are about 23 times smaller at the median (237 against 5,504 on HotpotQA dev, derived). A gain
   here says nothing about scale.
3. **Each question brings its own distractors.** In the pooled corpus they are hard negatives for
   their own question and ordinary paragraphs for the others.
4. **One hop against 2-4 hop questions.** D7 shows it: the gain is 89 % from 2-paragraph
   questions, small on 3-paragraph questions, and absent on 4-paragraph questions, where the hop
   systems' gold recall is slightly below Dense + BM25's. That is a limit of the design, not a
   failure of transfer.
5. **A frozen transfer is a lower bound on what fitting could give, and D9 an upper bound.** The
   honest figure for a corpus without labels is the frozen one; the gap here is at most 45
   questions (exploratory).
6. **One benchmark more, not generality.** Two Wikipedia benchmarks agreeing is evidence, not proof,
   that the mechanism is general.
7. **The source is a mirror.** The Hugging Face copy's files are held to their bytes and SHA-256;
   that they are byte-identical to the official release is not verified, and the license was not
   read at the pin (author decision 1).
8. **Near-duplicate gold.** 72 gold units (172 questions) have a near-identical twin that is not
   marked; absolute figures are slightly understated for every system (author decision 5).
9. **Long units.** 56 units by GLiNER's words (135 by its sub-word tokens) exceed GLiNER's window
   and are truncated; 3 units failed extraction (`bounds`) and 1,026 carry no entity. Their effect
   on the hop is not measured; it is at most a small share of the corpus (0.13 %, 0.003 % and
   1.01 %, derived).
10. **The invoiced cost is not yet known.** The session total from RunPod Billing is pending; the
    attributable 0.1395 USD covers the two build steps only.
11. **The HotpotQA column is not a like-for-like control.** `test-11` is HotpotQA train questions
    that BGE-small saw in fine-tuning, over 5.2 M paragraphs; MuSiQue validation is neither. Only
    direction and order compare (D6).
12. **Absolute MuSiQue figures are not compared with the literature.** The corpus here pools every
    train and validation paragraph (101,962 units), the metric is Full Support within a token
    budget, and the systems are untrained on MuSiQue; published MuSiQue retrieval figures differ in
    at least corpus, metric and supervision. No such comparison is made.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Established (measured, one frozen pass, nothing fitted on MuSiQue):**

- With every weight and `α` frozen at its HotpotQA value, **P14 beats Dense + BM25 on MuSiQue by
  +9.81 pp** Full Support @2,048 (294 wins, 57 losses, p = 1.3e-39), and **P10-C by +3.81 pp**
  (151 / 59, p = 1.7e-10).
- **The query-blind Entity Hop transfers too:** P10-C beats Dense + BM25 by +6.00 pp (189 / 44,
  p = 1.3e-22), a larger step than on HotpotQA `test-11` (+4.12 pp).
- **Every step of the ladder adds on both benchmarks**, in the same direction; the order of the
  steps differs.
- The relevance-ordered hop still lists more of the gold Dense misses than the query-blind hop
  (1,167 against 975 at depth 100), from the same candidate set.
- **The gain is a 2-paragraph gain.** On 3-paragraph questions it is small; on 4-paragraph
  questions no system retrieves the full evidence and the hop does not help.
- **Interpretation:** the hop's gain on HotpotQA does not depend only on HotpotQA's
  first-paragraph hyperlink construction. Whether MuSiQue's composed questions share a similar
  entity-link structure between their paragraphs is not measured.

**Not established:**

- anything about another kind of text (Phase 16) or about scale (a 101,962-paragraph corpus);
- that the mechanism is general: this is a second Wikipedia benchmark;
- any use for questions needing 3 or more paragraphs: the design has one seed and one hop, and the
  anti-objectives excluded more;
- anything beside a stronger Dense retriever;
- latency at scale: the corpus is 51 times smaller than FullWiki;
- the invoiced cost of the session (pending).
