# Spec Phase 10: Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop at FullWiki scale

> Functional spec — WHAT is measured and WHY. The HOW goes in the plan (`10.0_*.md`).
> Status: **approved by the author 2026-09-24 and frozen**. A change to any rule below is a
> written deviation `10.Y_*.md`.

---

## Problem and objective

**Problem.** Phase 9 measured the two complements of Dense separately over the 5,233,329-paragraph
HotpotQA FullWiki corpus. On the 5,405 retrieval-unseen questions, Dense + BM25 reached 61.30 %
Full Support @2,048 and Dense + Entity Hop 60.09 %. Their per-question outcomes disagree often:
371 questions go the Entity Hop's way and 436 go BM25's. A system that is merely as good as
Dense + BM25 has no reason to exist. The first question is whether the existing entity signal adds
anything **on top of** BM25.

**Question.**

> Does adding the frozen Phase 9 Entity Hop as a third fused component make Dense + BM25 retrieve
> the full multi-hop evidence more often at FullWiki scale?

**Objective.** One held-out comparison: P10-C (Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop, weights fitted on dev)
against P10-B (Dense + BM25, frozen). Both run on the laptop over the Phase 9 artifacts, with
their query latency measured in the same pass. A terminal label is derived mechanically.

This is **step 1 of 2**. Step 2, a later phase with its own spec, asks whether a *better use of the
entities* beats Dense + BM25: hub exclusion, the rarest entities only, and entities read from the
question. Its held-out questions are drawn and frozen here, and never touched by Phase 10.

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — Dev is the 7,405 HotpotQA validation questions

They were Phase 9's held-out set. Phase 10 **uses them as dev**. That is legitimate: they are
fitted on for a new system and never again reported as held-out evidence. The Phase 9 cohorts
and gold mapping (`data/phase9/questions.json`, question digest and mapping digest) are reused
unchanged.

### D2 — Fresh questions from HotpotQA train, level "hard"

BGE-small was fine-tuned on HotpotQA. The C-Pack paper (arXiv 2309.07597) lists it among the
supervised datasets: "NLI, FEVER, NQ, HotpotQA, Quora, StackExchange Duplicates and MEDI". Train
questions are therefore not unseen by Dense. The official HotpotQA test set publishes no gold, and
no other source of fresh HotpotQA questions exists. So the held-out questions come from train,
restricted to level `hard`, the level of the validation questions (the plan verifies that from the
release). The contamination is measured by D3 before any held-out question is used.

From the HotpotQA train release (pinned by URL, bytes and SHA-256 recorded at download),
`random.Random(10)` samples, **without replacement and at once**, three disjoint sets of hard
questions, in this order:

| Set | Size | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| `probe` | 1,000 | D3 contamination probe only; never a result |
| `test-10` | 5,000 | Phase 10 held-out |
| `test-11` | 5,000 | reserved for the step-2 phase; frozen by qid and digest, no retrieval in Phase 10 |

Fewer than 11,000 hard questions in the release → `DATA_STOP`. Gold titles are resolved under the
Phase 9 D4 rule (exact title, then a unique normalized title). Unresolved gold stays in the
denominator as a miss. More than 1 % unresolved in any set → `DATA_STOP`.

### D3 — Contamination probe, before the held-out set is opened

Dense alone (P10-A) runs on `probe` and on dev. Let `probe_fs` be Dense's Full Support @2,048 on
`probe` and `dev_fs` its figure on the 7,405 dev questions (Phase 9 recorded 4,125 / 7,405 =
55.70 %).

```text
probe_fs - dev_fs > 5.0 percentage points  ->  DATA_STOP (train questions too contaminated)
otherwise                                   ->  continue; the difference is reported
```

The 5-point margin is about three times the sampling standard error of a 1,000-question
proportion near 0.56 (about 1.6 points). The bias this probe bounds works **against** P10-C: a
Dense that recalls its training pairs leaves less for any complement to add. A positive result is
therefore conservative, and a null one is reported with the probe difference beside it.

### D4 — Reproduction gate on the laptop

Before any fit, the Phase 9 systems P9-A, P9-B and P9-C run on the laptop over the 7,405 dev
questions. They must reproduce the Phase 9 Full Support @2,048 counts exactly:

```text
P9-A Dense           4,125 / 7,405
P9-B Dense + BM25    4,536 / 7,405
P9-C Dense + Entity  4,441 / 7,405
```

A miss → stop and ask. No deviation is improvised.

### D5 — The P10-C fit, on dev only

- Grid: every weight triple `(w_dense, w_bm25, w_entity)` with each weight a multiple of 0.1 and
  the three summing to 1 (66 points), `weighted` scheme, min-max normalization per component.
- Objective: Full Support @2,048 on the 7,405 dev questions. Ties are broken by gold recall
  @2,048, then by the larger `w_dense`, then the larger `w_bm25`.
- **Dev gate.** Two outcomes stop the phase with `DEV_STOP`, without opening `test-10`:
  - the fit puts `w_entity = 0`;
  - its dev Full Support @2,048 does not exceed P10-B's dev count (4,536) by **at least one
    question**.

### D6 — Primary endpoint and test

Full Support @2,048 on `test-10`, P10-C against P10-B. One two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial on
the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
THREE_WAY_SUPPORTED   wins > losses and p < 0.05
THREE_WAY_REGRESSION  losses > wins and p < 0.05
THREE_WAY_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DATA_STOP             D2 or D3
DEV_STOP              D5 dev gate
```

The label is written by code. Nothing else can change it.

### D7 — Latency, reported, not gated

Each system's `retrieve` wall time per question is measured in the same pass on the laptop. It
covers Dense scoring, BM25, the Entity Hop and fusion, and excludes question encoding, as in
Phase 9. The report gives mean, median, p95, max and the ratio P10-C / P10-B. There is no ceiling:
the question is whether the ratio stays in the same order of magnitude. The host is recorded.

## User stories

### HU-1 — Frozen question sets

**As** the author, **I want** the three train samples drawn, gold-mapped and frozen before any
retrieval on them, **so that** no choice can depend on them.

- [ ] The train release is pinned (URL, bytes, SHA-256); the hard count is recorded.
- [ ] `probe`, `test-10` and `test-11` are disjoint, of sizes 1,000 / 5,000 / 5,000, drawn with
      `random.Random(10)`, each with a qid digest and a mapping digest; any overlap with the 7,405
      validation qids is zero.
- [ ] Unresolved gold per set is recorded; above 1 % → `DATA_STOP`.
- [ ] The question vectors for the new sets use the pinned BGE revision, in a cache separate from
      Phase 9's (the `question_cache_key` gotcha).

### HU-2 — Laptop reproduction and contamination probe

**As** the author, **I want** proof that the laptop computes what the pod computed, and a measure of
the train contamination, **so that** the held-out figure means what it says.

- [ ] D4 reproduces the three Phase 9 dev counts exactly, or the phase stops.
- [ ] D3 is computed and recorded, with its label, before `test-10` is loaded.

### HU-3 — Three-component fusion fitted on dev

**As** the author, **I want** the three-way weights chosen on dev alone, **so that** the held-out
comparison is honest.

- [ ] The full 66-point curve is recorded, with the selected point and the tie rule applied.
- [ ] The dev gate is applied by code. `DEV_STOP` leaves `test-10` unopened.
- [ ] The selection is committed before the held-out pass.

### HU-4 — One held-out pass

**As** the author, **I want** P10-A, P10-B and P10-C measured once on `test-10`, **so that** the
answer is one number with its test.

- [ ] Runs only after the author's explicit authorization, and refuses a second start.
- [ ] Per-question outcomes are kept for the paired test. The budget metrics are recorded at 512,
      1,024, 2,048 and 4,096 tokens, and FS/GPR at @2, @5, @10 and @20.
- [ ] Latency is recorded per D7.
- [ ] `outcome.json` records wins, losses, ties, the delta in points, the exact p and the label.

### HU-5 — Results

**As** the author, **I want** `10.results.md` with the answer, the probe difference, the latency
comparison and the limitations, **so that** the step-2 phase starts from a measured bar.

- [ ] Every value is labelled measured, derived or projection.
- [ ] `docs/research_summary.md` is updated only if the label is `THREE_WAY_SUPPORTED` or
      `THREE_WAY_REGRESSION`.

## Data contracts

- **`data/phase10/questions.json`**: source pin, hard count, seed, and for each set the qids,
  question digest, mapping digest, unresolved count, and per question the gold unit ids and their
  resolution. The existing `Question` fields are reused.
- **`data/phase10/reproduction.json`**: the three dev counts against the Phase 9 counts, with a
  pass/fail result.
- **`data/phase10/probe.json`**: `probe_fs`, `dev_fs`, the difference, the 5.0 margin and the
  verdict.
- **`data/phase10/fit.json`**: the grid, the curve, the selected weights, the tie rule, the dev
  gate verdict and the P10-B dev count.
- **`data/phase10/run-<system>.json`** and **`outcomes-<system>.jsonl.gz`**: the Phase 9 run
  contract (metrics, latency, provenance: corpus, questions, vectors, BM25, extraction, entity
  index, weights, commit, host).
- **`data/phase10/outcome.json`**: primary test, label and stop reasons.

Every artifact is JSON or NPZ. No pickle.

---

## Restrictions

- **R1.** No rented compute and no paid API. Everything runs on the laptop.
- **R2.** Phase 9 artifacts are read-only. Phase 10 writes only under `data/phase10/` and its own
  question-vector cache.
- **R3.** Nothing is fitted or selected on `probe`, `test-10` or `test-11`.
- **R4.** P10-B keeps the frozen 0.5 / 0.5 weights, and the Entity Hop keeps its frozen
  definition.

---

## Anti-objectives

Phase 10 does **not**:

- change the entities: no hub cap, no rarest-k, no question entities, no normalization change and
  no entity types (all step 2 or later);
- link entities to titles or use hyperlinks;
- refit P10-B or change any Phase 9 weight;
- re-extract, re-embed the corpus or rebuild the entity index;
- add query awareness, extra seeds or extra hops;
- train anything, add a cross-encoder or put an LLM in retrieval;
- open or retrieve on `test-11`.
