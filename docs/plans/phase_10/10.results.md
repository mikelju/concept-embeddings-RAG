# Phase 10 results — Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop at FullWiki scale

> Spec: [`10.spec.md`](10.spec.md) (approved and frozen 2026-09-24) · Plan:
> [`10.0_three_way.md`](10.0_three_way.md) (including its "Corrections")
>
> The single held-out pass ran once, on 2026-09-24, on the author's laptop, after the author's
> explicit authorization at stop point 1. It measured P10-A, P10-B and P10-C on the 5,000 `test-10`
> questions over the 5,233,329-paragraph Phase 9 FullWiki corpus. The three-way weights were fitted
> on the 7,405 HotpotQA validation questions (dev) and committed (`bbe1a3d`, 12:10 UTC) before the
> pass marker was written (14:38 UTC). `test-11` was drawn and frozen, and never retrieved on.

Every value is labelled: **measured** (read from an artifact of this phase), **derived** (arithmetic
on measured values) or **projection**. Percentages are rounded half up from the exact counts.

---

## Result

**Terminal state: `THREE_WAY_SUPPORTED`.**

Source: `data/phase10/outcome.json`, written mechanically by `cer p10-outcome` from the two run
files.

Primary endpoint: Full Support @2,048 tokens on `test-10` (5,000 HotpotQA train questions, level
`hard`), P10-C against P10-B.

| System | Full Support @2,048 | Share | Label |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P10-B Dense + BM25 (0.5 / 0.5, frozen Phase 3 fit) | 3,129 / 5,000 | 62.58 % | measured |
| P10-C Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop (0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2, fitted on dev) | **3,325 / 5,000** | **66.50 %** | measured |
| Difference | +196 | **+3.92 pp** | derived |

```text
exact two-sided McNemar: wins 306, losses 110, ties 4,584   p = 1.9e-22   (alpha = 0.05)
```

The answer to the phase question: **yes.** Adding the frozen Phase 9 Entity Hop as a third fused
component makes Dense + BM25 retrieve the full two-paragraph evidence more often at FullWiki scale.
The entity signal is not redundant with BM25. At the primary budget it gains on 306 questions for
every 110 it loses.

The P10-A reference row (Dense alone) reaches 2,916 / 5,000 (58.32 %, measured). It decides nothing.

## Latency (D7, reported, not gated)

`Retriever.retrieve` wall time per question, question vector served from its cache; it includes
Dense scoring, BM25, the Entity Hop and fusion, and excludes question encoding, as in Phase 9. All
three systems ran in the same process, one after another, on the same host.

| System | Mean | Median | p95 | Max | Label |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| P10-A Dense | 259 ms | 224 ms | 493 ms | 1,520 ms | measured |
| P10-B Dense + BM25 | 336 ms | 333 ms | 391 ms | 538 ms | measured |
| P10-C Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop | 446 ms | 367 ms | 838 ms | 16,537 ms | measured |
| **Ratio P10-C / P10-B** | **1.33** | 1.10 | 2.14 | 30.7 | derived |

The mean ratio (1.33, written by code in `outcome.json`) keeps P10-C in the same order of magnitude
as P10-B. The tail does not: one question took 16.5 s, against a 0.45 s mean. Phase 9 saw the same
shape for the Entity Hop (max 6.4 s against a 0.31 s mean) and attributed it, most probably, to
hub-dominated candidate sets. That attribution is not re-measured here.

**Host (measured, recorded in each run):** Windows 11 ARM64 (10.0.26200), Qualcomm Snapdragon X
Elite, 12 cores, 33,896,992,768 bytes of RAM (31.6 GiB), Python 3.12.10, torch 2.13.0+cpu, no GPU.

**Wall time of the pass (measured from the artifacts' timestamps):** pass marker 14:38:38 UTC after
loading and verifying the inputs; P10-A written 15:00, P10-B 15:28, P10-C 16:06. About 1 h 28 min of
measurement, inside the plan's 1-2 h projection. Peak memory was not recorded by the stage.

## Contamination probe (D3)

BGE-small was fine-tuned on HotpotQA (C-Pack, arXiv 2309.07597), so the train questions are not
unseen by Dense. The probe bounded that before `test-10` was opened.

| Quantity | Value | Label |
|---|---:|---|
| Dense FS @2,048 on `probe` (1,000 train questions) | 586 / 1,000 = 58.60 % | measured |
| Dense FS @2,048 on dev (7,405 validation questions) | 4,125 / 7,405 = 55.71 % | measured |
| Difference | +2.89 pp | derived |
| Margin | 5.0 pp | spec |
| Verdict | continue (no `DATA_STOP`) | measured |
| Dense FS @2,048 on `test-10` | 2,916 / 5,000 = 58.32 %, +2.61 pp over dev | measured / derived |

(The spec quotes the dev figure as 55.70 %; 4,125 / 7,405 = 55.706 %. The verdict used the exact
value.)

**The bias it implies.** Dense reads the train questions about 2.6-2.9 points better than the
validation questions. The spec's reading: a Dense that recalls its training pairs leaves less for
any complement to add, so this bias works **against** P10-C, and the supported result is
conservative in that respect. Two cautions stay with it. The difference is a difference of question
populations, not a measured memorization effect, and the gain the complements add was measured on
those same train questions. Counting the sampling error of both sides (dev's
7,405 questions and the train set), the probe's 2.9-point gap is about 1.7 standard errors and
`test-10`'s 2.6-point gap about 2.9 (derived). The shift is real, and small against the 5-point
margin.

## The dev fit (D5)

The 66 convex triples at step 0.1, scored on the cached dev lists (`data/phase10/fit.json`,
committed in `bbe1a3d` before the pass). Selected: **dense 0.5 / bm25 0.3 / entity-hop 0.2**, 4,801 /
7,405 dev Full Support @2,048 against P10-B's 4,536 (+265, +3.58 pp, derived). The selected point is
unique at its count, so the tie rule did not decide. Dev gate: passed (`w_entity` > 0, gain > 1).

The curve is single-peaked in `w_entity`, with a flat top between 0.1 and 0.3 (4,723-4,801). Dense

below the best two-way point on dev (0.6 / 0.4, 4,541); P10-B was not refitted, as the spec
requires.

**Dev against held-out.** The gain over P10-B was +3.58 pp on dev and +3.92 pp on `test-10`
(derived). The fit did not overfit the 7,405 dev questions in any visible way. The populations
differ (validation against train), so this is descriptive.

## Limitations

1. **Train questions, a fine-tuned Dense.** The held-out questions come from HotpotQA train, which
   BGE-small saw during fine-tuning. The probe bounds the effect (+2.89 pp, within the 5-point
   margin) and argues it works against P10-C, but the absolute figures are not comparable with
   validation-set figures in the literature or in Phase 9.
2. **Dev is the Phase 9 held-out set.** The 7,405 validation questions are now fitted on. They can
   no longer serve as held-out evidence for any system in this line.
3. **Provenance recording gap in the three run files.** Their `provenance` block kept fields from
   the Phase 9 chain unchanged: `question_digest` and `mapping_digest` are the dev ones
   (`aaa0bc26…`, `e893fb6d…`), `embedding.question_cache_key` is the dev cache key (`4894aceb…`)
   and `weights` lists the Phase 9 P9-B / P9-C weights. It is a recording gap, not a measurement
   one:
   - `load_set` recomputed and matched the test-10 digests before the pass;
   - the question backend read the test-10 key (`a94350f8…`);
   - each run's `fusion` block records the weights actually used (P10-B 0.5 / 0.5; P10-C
     0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2);
   - the three outcome files hold exactly the 5,000 test-10 qids and none of test-11.

   The runs keep what they recorded. The producer is fixed prospectively (commit `81dd437`, with a
   test).
4. **The latency tail.** P10-C's maximum is 16.5 s, and P10-A's 1.5 s maximum exceeds P10-B's,
   probably a cold start as the first system of the pass. Neither is explained by a measurement
   here. The spec reports latency without a ceiling.
5. **One host, one run.** The latencies are those of this laptop and this brute-force CPU
   implementation, not of a tuned service. Peak memory was not recorded.
6. **The train pin is the frozen file, not an upstream revision.** The SHA-256 covers the slimmed
   JSON assembled from the HuggingFace rows endpoint (plan correction 1). No dataset commit was
   recorded, and the file is not in git. If it is lost, a re-fetch after an upstream change could
   produce another draw. The frozen `questions.json` (qids and digests per set) stays the record of
   what was drawn.
7. **`outcome.json` has no `stop_reasons` field.** The spec's contract lists one. Here it would
   have been empty: the stop states are enforced upstream, and none fired. The producer now writes
   it prospectively.
8. **The "pre-spec" diagnostic overlapped the approval.** Its log runs 11:18-12:28 local time,
   and the spec was approved at 11:50. It is dev-only and fitted and selected nothing. Loading
   GLiNER in it touched a directory timestamp under `data/phase9/models/`, and no file there
   changed.
9. **Only the frozen hop.** Phase 10 changes nothing in the entities (no hub control, no rarest-k,
   no question entities). Whether a better use of them adds more is the step-2 question.

## Next research decision

Phase 10 answers its question: the frozen, query-blind, one-hop Entity Hop adds statistically
supported value **on top of** Dense + BM25 at FullWiki scale (+3.92 pp Full Support @2,048, 306 wins
against 110 losses), for a 1.33× mean latency.

This changes the bar for step 2. The spec framed step 2 as "does a better use of the entities beat
Dense + BM25". With P10-C measured, the natural bar for a better entity signal is P10-C itself.
Whether step 2 compares against P10-B, P10-C or both is a decision for its spec.

Inputs ready for it:

- `test-11` (5,000 hard train questions, frozen by qid and digest, never retrieved on);
- the pre-spec dev diagnostic in `data/phase10/pre-spec-diagnostic/reach_diagnostic.json`;
- the latency tail above, which a hub-control variant would also address.

Which direction comes next is the author's decision.
