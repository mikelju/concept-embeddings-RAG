# Spec Phase 9: HotpotQA FullWiki — literature-comparable scale

> Functional specification — WHAT is measured and WHY, not how it is implemented.
> The HOW belongs to the implementation plan (`9.0_fullwiki.md`), written after this document is approved.
>
> **STATUS: APPROVED by the author on 2026-09-23. Frozen: any change is a written deviation.**
>
> Phase 9 asks one scientific question. The corpus is large, but the retrieval method is not allowed
> to become more complicated merely to rescue a weak result.

---

## Problem and objective

**Problem.** Phases 5–7 established that one raw shared-entity hop can recover multi-hop evidence
that Dense retrieval misses, and that GLiNER can build that representation cheaply. But the
end-to-end Entity Hop result was measured on only **19,366 paragraphs**. At FullWiki scale,
millions of unrelated paragraphs can share the same surface entities, creating hubs and distractors
that did not exist at the original scale.

Deviation 8.1 measured the Dense side of that risk. BGE-small remained ahead of Qwen from 19,366
through 500,000 paragraphs, so Phase 9 carries BGE-small as the task-validated primary Dense
retriever. It did **not** measure Entity Hop at FullWiki scale.

**Objective.** Answer the master-plan question:

> **Does the simple Dense + Entity Hop architecture remain useful when retrieval is performed over
> the standard multi-million-paragraph HotpotQA FullWiki search space?**

The phase must produce:

1. a primary scale result on HotpotQA questions that have never contributed a retrieval score to
   this project;
2. a standard 7,405-question FullWiki-dev result table;
3. literature-comparable retrieval metrics, with a protocol ledger that prevents pooled-corpus
   GraphRAG numbers from being presented as FullWiki comparisons;
4. measured extraction, indexing, storage, memory, latency, candidate-count and hub statistics.

Positive, null, negative, `DATA_STOP` and `OPERATIONAL_STOP` are all valid completions.

---

## What changes and what stays fixed

The scientific variable is **corpus scale**:

```text
19,366-paragraph frozen pool
        ↓
complete official HotpotQA processed-Wikipedia / FullWiki corpus
```

The mechanism remains:

```text
question
  ↓
BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5
  ↓
P1
  ↓
raw GLiNER entities in P1
  ↓
one shared-entity hop
  ↓
Dense + Entity fusion
```

No richer graph structure is introduced.

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — FullWiki corpus identity

Phase 9 reuses the exact official processed-Wikipedia archive verified in deviation 8.1:

```text
archive
enwiki-20171001-pages-meta-current-withlinks-abstracts.tar.bz2

declared / measured bytes
1,553,565,403

declared / measured MD5
01edf64cd120ecc03a2745352779514c

local SHA-256
1acca1c5cc93c4890ea51091d2bad7c3ef6987aead127ab88728dc9e26555729
```

MD5 exists only to match the release checksum; SHA-256 is the local integrity record.
Deviation 8.1 recorded `page_checked: false`, and Phase 9 does not rewrite that historical fact.

**The complete archive is the corpus.** C100/C250/C500 from 8.1 are diagnostics, not Phase 9
corpora. The exact FullWiki unit count is measured from the archive rather than copied from a
third-party packaging.

### D2 — Indexing unit stays one complete Wikipedia intro paragraph

The existing contract remains:

```text
plaintext      = " ".join(sentences)
indexable_text = f"{title}. {plaintext}"
unit_id        = existing unit_id_for(...)
```

No sentence chunks, fixed token chunks, merging or truncation are introduced.

### D3 — Question cohorts

Phase 1 selected 2,000 questions with seed 42 from HotpotQA `distractor/validation`, split into
600 development and 1,400 historical test. The repository also carries the full **7,405-record**
validation source.

Phase 9 freezes these cohorts by qid before any Phase 9 retrieval result exists:

| Cohort | Expected size | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| `standard-dev` | 7,405 | Literature-comparability result |
| `historical-overlap` | 2,000 | Questions used in previous project retrieval work |
| `retrieval-unseen` | 5,405 | Primary scientific cohort |

```text
retrieval-unseen = standard-dev - exact Phase 1 2,000-qid subset
```

The counts are acceptance conditions. If qids do not reconcile exactly, Phase 9 stops at the data
gate.

“Retrieval-unseen” is deliberately narrow: these qids have not contributed a retrieval metric,
fusion decision, extractor decision or Dense-model decision in Phases 1–8.1. The full raw source
has previously been inspected for schema/link statistics, so the spec does not call them globally
untouched.

The candidate contexts shipped in HotpotQA's `fullwiki` dev JSON are **not** retrieval input.
Phase 9 searches the complete processed-Wikipedia corpus.

The hidden-label official FullWiki test set is outside this retrieval-only phase.

**Single evaluation pass.** The 5,405 `retrieval-unseen` questions act as a new held-out set, and the
7,405 include the 1,400 historical Phase 1 test questions. Phase 9 selects nothing, so no selection
is contaminated; even so, retrieval metrics over any cohort are computed **once**, after the corpus,
gold mapping, embeddings, BM25 index and entity index are frozen and digested, and only after the
author explicitly authorizes that pass. No cohort is rerun because of what the pass shows. Smoke tests
before that pass use synthetic or C19 fixtures, never the Phase 9 cohorts' metrics.

### D4 — Gold mapping is fixed before retrieval

For each of the 7,405 questions:

1. collapse supporting facts to distinct supporting article titles (verified locally: all 7,405
   have exactly two);
2. resolve each title to a FullWiki unit **by title**: first an exact title match; if none, a match
   on the deviation-8.1 `normalized_title` (NFC, whitespace collapse, casefold), accepted only if it
   identifies exactly one FullWiki unit;
3. a title with no match is **unmatched**; a title whose exact or normalized form identifies more
   than one unit is **ambiguous**.

This differs deliberately from the 8.1 reconciliation, which matched C19 paragraphs by title **and
text**: Phase 9 retrieves over FullWiki itself, so the gold unit is the FullWiki paragraph of the
supporting article, whatever text the distractor-setting copy carried. (8.1 found 1 of 1,194 dev
gold paragraphs without a title+text match.)

No fuzzy, edit-distance, embedding or LLM-assisted gold matching is allowed.

**Unresolved gold stays in the denominator.** A question with an unmatched or ambiguous gold title
is kept, and that gold unit counts as never retrieved for **every** system alike: the question can
still score gold recall on its other title but can never reach Full Support. No question is dropped
and no denominator changes.

**`DATA_STOP` ceiling:** if more than **74 questions (1% of 7,405)** have at least one unresolved
gold title, Phase 9 stops at the data gate. The ceiling is frozen here, before any mapping is run.
The unresolved count, titles and affected qids are reported either way.

### D5 — BGE-small is the only Dense model measured

```text
model         BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5
revision      5c38ec7c405ec4b44b94cc5a9bb96e735b38267a
dimension     384
query prompt  empty
normalized    yes
```

**Qwen is excluded from Phase 9 measurement.**

Deviation 8.1 already tested whether its Phase 8 deficit disappeared as the corpus grew to 500k and
returned `STABLE_RANKING`; the deficit widened. Re-embedding FullWiki with Qwen would duplicate
the expensive Dense path while reopening a retriever-comparison question that Phase 9 is not
asking. Its 8.1 curve remains contextual evidence only.

### D6 — GLiNER is the only entity extractor

The Phase 7 selection is frozen:

```text
model                 urchade/gliner_medium-v2.1
revision              40ec419335d09393f298636f471328b722c6da9e
labels                frozen Phase 7 five-label set
threshold             0.5
max_tokens            384
configuration_digest  2f7864661b8ce7ff
normalization          normalization-v1
```

Every FullWiki unit is attempted exactly once by the canonical extraction pass. Failures are
recorded by kind and rate.

spaCy is not added. Reopening the extractor at scale is explicitly deferred by the master plan.

### D7 — Entity Hop semantics are frozen

Phase 9 carries the existing `EntityHopStage` semantics:

- raw normalized entity forms;
- entity nodes only;
- P1 as the only Dense seed;
- one hop;
- inherited read depth;
- rarity `log(1 + N / (1 + df))`;
- positive scores only;
- deterministic ties;
- no padding;
- no canonicalization;
- no relations;
- no query-aware filtering;
- no hub/degree cap;
- no candidate pruning;
- no learned traversal;
- no second seed;
- no second hop.

Storage/index optimizations are allowed only if the ranking semantics remain exact. If hubs make the
exact mechanism operationally impractical, that is a result, not permission to prune after seeing it.

### D8 — No FullWiki tuning; fusion weights stay frozen

Phase 9 changes corpus scale only. Therefore no fusion search is run on the 7,405 questions or on
the 5,405 retrieval-unseen cohort.

Required systems:

```text
P9-A  Primary Dense
      BGE-small

P9-B  Dense + BM25
      weighted: dense 0.5 / bm25 0.5

P9-C  Dense + Entity Hop (GLiNER)
      weighted: dense 0.7 / entity-hop 0.3
```

Those are inherited, previously selected weights. No alternate weight or RRF scheme is tried after
a FullWiki result is visible.

### D9 — Primary endpoint: Full Support @2,048 tokens on `retrieval-unseen`

For a question with two gold paragraphs:

```text
Full Support @B = 1 iff both gold unit ids fit in the ranked list truncated to a budget of B
                  tokens under the unchanged Phase 1 budget ruler
                 0 otherwise
```

The primary comparison is:

```text
P9-C Dense + Entity Hop
vs
P9-A Dense
```

on the 5,405 `retrieval-unseen` questions at **B = 2,048 tokens**.

This is the project's metric of record, the objective the frozen 0.7 / 0.3 weight was fitted for in
Phase 6 and re-confirmed in Phase 7, and the budget at which the Phase 6/7 gain was measured.
A rank cut-off of K = 2 is not used as the primary endpoint: Entity Hop candidates are by
construction drawn from outside Dense's top 10, so K = 2 would largely test the fusion weight's
behaviour at the very top rather than whether the mechanism survives scale. The rank-based
literature metrics (@2/@5/@10/@20) are reported descriptively (HU-6).

### D10 — One inferential test

The primary outcome is binary and paired. Use one two-sided exact McNemar/binomial test on
discordant questions:

```text
wins   = P9-C succeeds, P9-A fails
losses = P9-A succeeds, P9-C fails
alpha  = 0.05
```

Terminal scientific label:

```text
SCALE_SUPPORTED
  wins > losses and p < 0.05

SCALE_REGRESSION
  losses > wins and p < 0.05

SCALE_NOT_SUPPORTED
  otherwise
```

The test is run on Full Support @2,048 tokens (D9). All secondary metrics are descriptive. No
alternative K, budget or subgroup can overwrite the terminal label.

P9-C versus P9-B is reported because Phase 6 established a BM25-replacement result, but it is
secondary here. The report must say clearly if Entity Hop remains complementary to Dense but no
longer matches the BM25 control.

### D11 — Data and operational stops

```text
DATA_STOP
  corpus identity or question IDs violate the frozen data contract, or more than 74 questions
  have an unresolved gold title (D4)

OPERATIONAL_STOP
  a required exact system cannot be built or measured inside the envelope below without
  changing retrieval semantics
```

**Frozen envelope:**

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Host class | one rented RunPod NVIDIA RTX 4090 (the 8.1 measurement class), Linux x86_64 |
| Hard spend ceiling | **25 USD** total rented-session billing for Phase 9 |
| Query cost | mean **<= 1.0 s** per question per system over the evaluation pass (the 8.1 ceiling) |
| Memory | every index used by a system fits in the host's RAM / VRAM as rented |

A rough, **unmeasured** expectation for orientation only: BGE at 8.1's measured 2,136 paragraphs/s
is about 41 min for ~5.2M units; GLiNER's Phase 7 linear projection is 8.56 h / 6.34 USD. The plan
records the exact hourly rate, storage and download checklist. If a stop fires, completed artifacts
and measured bottleneck data are preserved. No approximation is introduced silently.

## Metrics and exact semantics

### Fixed-token-budget metrics (primary family)

The Phase 1 harness and budget ruler, unchanged:

```text
budgets = 512, 1024, 2048, 4096
metrics = Full Support, Gold Paragraph Recall, Context Precision
```

The historical BGE budget tokenizer remains fixed. **Full Support @2,048 tokens on
`retrieval-unseen` is the sole primary endpoint.**

### Rank-based literature metrics (descriptive)

```text
FS@K(q)  = 1 if gold(q) ⊆ topK(q), else 0
GPR@K(q) = |gold(q) ∩ topK(q)| / 2
```

These are the metrics the FullWiki retrieval literature reports; they carry no decision.

### Paired primary test

Report P9-A successes, P9-C successes, wins, losses, ties, absolute percentage-point delta, exact
two-sided p-value and the D10 terminal label. No subgroup analysis can change that label.

## Literature protocol ledger

The master plan requires the standard metric set to be fixed only after checking the principal
papers. That check changes how several names in the roadmap must be used.

**Verification status:** the paper details below (metric cut-offs, question counts, corpus sizes)
were gathered while drafting and are **not yet verified against the sources**. What this spec
fixes is the rule and the rank-based metric family; every external figure and protocol detail is
checked against the paper itself when `9.results.md` is written, and a detail that cannot be
verified is not shown.

### Direct FullWiki anchors

**MDR — Xiong et al., ICLR 2021, _Answering Complex Open-Domain Questions with Multi-Hop Dense
Retrieval_.**

- Open-domain HotpotQA against a large Wikipedia corpus.
- Reports retrieval `R@2`, `R@10`, `R@20`.
- This is the principal historical numerical anchor.
- MDR ranks passage sequences; Phase 9 returns a flat list. `9.results.md` must state the exact
  mapping rather than assume the metric implementations are identical.

**PromptRank — Khalifa et al., ACL 2023, _Few-shot Reranking for Multi-hop QA via Language Model
Prompting_.**

- Reports the same HotpotQA `R@2/R@10/R@20` family alongside MDR/DrKIT baselines.
- Useful as a later open-domain retrieval anchor, with supervision/reranking differences stated.

**MoPo — Xia et al., COLING 2025, _Momentum Posterior Regularization for Multi-hop Dense
Retrieval_.**

- Reports FullWiki HotpotQA at `R@2/R@20/R@50/R@100` and reranked results.
- Useful as a modern dense multi-hop reference; it is trained and multi-hop by construction.

These papers are cited, not reimplemented.

### Related HotpotQA results that are not direct FullWiki comparisons

**KG²RAG — NAACL 2025.** Its paper names a `HotpotQA-Full` setting, but describes a corpus of
**66,581 Wikipedia documents**, not the official multi-million-paragraph FullWiki corpus. Its
precision/recall/F1 results are related evidence, not direct Phase 9 numbers.

**HippoRAG-family / HGRAG / LinearRAG-style evaluations.** Recent graph-augmented work commonly
inherits the HippoRAG/IRCoT protocol: **1,000 sampled questions** and a pooled HotpotQA corpus on
the order of **9k passages**. That is an architecture benchmark, not FullWiki.

**SAG — Wu et al., 2026.** SAG explicitly follows the HippoRAG 2 pooled setup: 1,000 development
questions and roughly 9.8k HotpotQA passages; it reports Recall@1/2/5/10. Its shared-entity
query-time expansion is mechanistically relevant, but its HotpotQA number is contextual only.

### Rule for external numbers in `9.results.md`

Every external result shown later carries:

```text
paper
question set / count
corpus definition / size
retrieval unit
metric definition
supervision/training note
comparability = direct / partial / no
```

A single leaderboard mixing FullWiki and pooled-corpus results is prohibited.

## Restrictions

### R1 — SEC-033 is fixed before FullWiki reuse

Deviation 8.1's audit found that `probe_layout` can hand an oversized line (bounded by the member
ceiling) to `json.loads` before applying the canonical per-line limit. Before Phase 9 parses FullWiki:

- the schema probe must enforce the same per-line ceiling as canonical record parsing;
- a regression test must prove an oversized probe line is refused.

This is a prerequisite security fix, not a retrieval change.

### R2 — No hidden corpus reduction

No HotpotQA top-10 candidate list, C500 prefix, lexical prefilter or per-question candidate pool may
replace the complete FullWiki corpus for P9-A/P9-B/P9-C.

### R3 — No semantic approximation of Entity Hop

Changing storage/layout is allowed. Changing eligibility or scores through degree caps, pruning,
sampling or approximate structural expansion is not. Any such change requires a spec deviation
before measurement.

### R4 — No FullWiki tuning

No Phase 9 result may cause a change to model/extractor revisions, labels, thresholds, weights,
entity node types, number of seeds/hops, normalization or the primary endpoint.

### R5 — Historical budget ruler stays historical

Fixed-token-budget metrics use the same historical tokenizer/ruler so longitudinal values retain
their meaning.

### R6 — Expensive compute requires an approved envelope

Before rented FullWiki compute starts, the implementation plan records host/GPU class, storage,
hourly rate, expected duration, the D11 hard spend ceiling (25 USD) and artifact-download checklist.
The run starts only after author approval. The single evaluation pass over the question cohorts
(D3) is a second, separate authorization.

### R7 — Measured versus projected is explicit

Every cost/time value is labelled `measured`, `derived from measured billing`, `linear projection`
or `external published number`.

### R8 — Headline provenance is complete

Every headline result resolves to code commit, corpus identity, question-set identity, model/
extractor revisions, artifact digests, frozen weights, ranking depth, host and run artifact.

## Interpretation rules

### `SCALE_SUPPORTED`

Permitted claim:

> On the retrieval-unseen HotpotQA FullWiki cohort, the frozen one-hop GLiNER Entity signal
> provides statistically supported complementary retrieval value beyond BGE-small.

Whether it remains competitive with Dense+BM25 is stated separately.

### `SCALE_NOT_SUPPORTED`

The Phase 6/7 complementarity is not supported at FullWiki scale under the frozen mechanism.
Secondary metrics diagnose; they do not relabel the result.

### `SCALE_REGRESSION`

The frozen Entity Hop significantly harms the primary FullWiki endpoint relative to Dense. No
within-phase rescue is attempted.

### `DATA_STOP`

Report the corpus/gold incompatibility exactly. Do not publish altered-denominator retrieval scores.

### `OPERATIONAL_STOP`

Report which exact representation/path exceeded the approved envelope and preserve completed
measurements. Operational failure of the frozen mechanism is itself a scale result.

## Literature basis checked for this spec

- **HotpotQA official release**: the FullWiki dev file is the validation question set with contexts
  replaced by the authors' own top-10 retrieval; users are explicitly encouraged to replace those
  contexts with their own IR output. The processed Wikipedia release is the FullWiki corpus.
- **Xiong et al., ICLR 2021 — MDR**: FullWiki/open-domain HotpotQA; `R@2/R@10/R@20`.
- **Khalifa et al., ACL 2023 — PromptRank**: same HotpotQA retrieval metric family alongside MDR.
- **Xia et al., COLING 2025 — MoPo**: FullWiki HotpotQA retrieval at
  `R@2/R@20/R@50/R@100`.
- **Zhu et al., NAACL 2025 — KG²RAG**: a `HotpotQA-Full` experiment over 66,581 Wikipedia
  documents; related but not the official multi-million-paragraph FullWiki search space.
- **HippoRAG-family recent graph work**: typically 1,000 questions and pooled ~9k HotpotQA
  passages.
- **Wu et al., 2026 — SAG**: explicitly follows that pooled protocol; 1,000 questions, roughly
  9.8k HotpotQA passages, Recall@1/2/5/10.

Those protocol differences are part of the experimental validity.
