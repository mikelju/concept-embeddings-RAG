> The single evaluation pass ran once, on 2026-09-23, after the author's explicit authorization
> at stop point 2. It measured all 7,405 HotpotQA validation questions at FullWiki scale: the
> 5,405 never used before in this project, the 1,400 historical test questions (read at pool
> scale in earlier phases) and the 600 historical dev questions. Nothing was fitted or chosen on
> any of them in Phase 9: the fusion weights are the frozen Phase 3 / Phase 7 fits, and the hop
> implementation was fixed by the probe over 100 historical dev questions before the pass.

## Result

**Terminal state: `SCALE_SUPPORTED`.**

Source: `data/phase9/outcome.json`, derived mechanically by `cer fullwiki-outcome` from the three
run files.

Primary endpoint: Full Support @2,048 tokens on the **5,405 `retrieval-unseen` questions**, over
the complete HotpotQA processed Wikipedia (**5,233,329 paragraphs**).

| System | Full Support @2,048 | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| P9-A Dense (BGE-small) | 3,006 / 5,405 | 55.62 % |
| P9-C Dense + Entity Hop (GLiNER, 0.7 / 0.3) | **3,248 / 5,405** | **60.09 %** |
| Difference | +242 | **+4.48 pp** |

```text
exact two-sided McNemar: wins 374, losses 132, ties 4,899   p = 8.0e-28   (alpha = 0.05)
```

The permitted claim (spec, "Interpretation rules"):

> On the retrieval-unseen HotpotQA FullWiki cohort, the frozen one-hop GLiNER Entity signal
> provides statistically supported complementary retrieval value beyond BGE-small.

**Competitiveness with Dense + BM25, stated separately as the spec requires.** On the same
cohort P9-B (Dense + BM25, 0.5 / 0.5) reaches **3,313 / 5,405 (61.30 %)**, ahead of P9-C by
**1.20 pp** (wins 371, losses 436, exact p = 0.024). This comparison is descriptive and cannot
change the label. At Phase 7 dev scale the two hybrids were tied (518 / 600 each). At FullWiki
scale the frozen Entity Hop is **not** competitive with BM25 at the primary budget. It is at
4,096 tokens (67.0 % against 66.6 %).

### Scale reading against the historical pool

| | Phase 7 dev (19,366-paragraph pool, 600 q) | Phase 9 unseen (5.23M paragraphs, 5,405 q) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dense FS @2,048 | 81.17 % | 55.62 % |
| Dense + Entity Hop | 86.33 % | 60.09 % |
| Entity Hop gain | +5.17 pp | +4.48 pp |

The absolute level falls by about 25 points once the search space is the whole of Wikipedia.
The Entity Hop's gain over Dense is almost entirely kept. The questions and the corpora differ
between the two columns, so this is a descriptive comparison, not a paired one.

Each external figure below was read in the paper itself on 2026-09-24. A detail that could not be
verified there is not shown. No row is a leaderboard entry: the systems differ in supervision,

**Mapping to Phase 9.** Phase 9's `FS@K` is PromptRank's definition exactly: both gold paragraphs
in a flat top-K. MDR ranks passage pairs and counts at top k/2 pairs. MoPo's R@k counts a question
as recalled when any single gold document appears. Phase 9's retrievers are not trained on
HotpotQA, but they are not zero-shot either. The fusion weights were fitted on 600 HotpotQA
validation questions (Phase 3 / Phase 7 dev), and Phase 7 chose GLiNER on the same 600. Those
questions are part of the 7,405 `standard-dev` cohort used for this placement. The primary
`retrieval-unseen` cohort contains none of them.

**Descriptive placement.** On the 7,405 standard-dev questions, BGE-small Dense (untrained, no fusion) gets
FS@2 25.7, FS@10 48.4 and FS@20 54.9. PromptRank's unsupervised TF-IDF + BM25 with hyperlinks
gets 19.1 / 54.7 / 61.8. Trained MDR gets 65.9 / 77.5 / 80.2 under its pair-based counting.
Phase 9 claims no rank against them.

**Pooled protocols.** The HippoRAG-family and SAG numbers named in the spec were **not verified
in this pass**, so none is shown. The spec's rule stands: a ~9k-passage pooled result is never
presented as a FullWiki result.

**Ledger corrections against the spec's unverified draft:**
- **MoPo's R@k** measures at-least-one-gold-document recall, not both-gold recall. Its R@k figures
  are therefore not comparable, and only EM@k is partially comparable.
- **MDR's Table 1** does not state its question count, so none is attributed to it.

## Limitations

1. **Two hosts.** The probe ran on the build pod. The pass ran on a second RTX 4090 in another
   region, because RunPod reassigned the first pod's GPU while it was stopped. Both are the
   spec's host class. The latencies, however, come from different CPUs (48 against 256 reported)
   because:
2. **Query-time Dense scoring runs on the CPU.** The pass recorded 0 VRAM: brute-force Dense
   scoring is numpy. The GPU served only the build (BGE, GLiNER). The query latencies measure
   this implementation on that host, not a GPU search.
3. **The probe was recorded at `9abdc26` and the pass ran at `b7a28f1`.** The author kept the
   probe (plan correction 6). The fix changed how inputs load, not what is timed. That explains
   the probe's higher peak RSS: it loaded the inputs twice.
4. **P9-C's latency tail.** The maximum was 6.4 s against a 0.31 s mean, most probably from
   hub-dominated candidate sets (p99 227k candidates). The ceiling is on the mean, as the spec
   fixes it, but the tail would matter for a service.
5. **Determinism of the extraction.** GLiNER at batch 8 on CUDA can move a span sitting exactly
   on the threshold between devices (recorded in `extraction.json`). The extraction artifact is
   the record, and everything downstream of it is deterministic.
6. **Literature comparability is partial at best.** See the ledger. No external number was
   reproduced.
7. **Two recording gaps in the derived artifacts, neither changing the result.**
   - `probe.json` keeps per-question timings only for the hop it selected. The reference hop
     survives as its mean (0.967 s).
   - In `outcome.json`, the descriptive `secondary_entity_vs_bm25` block reuses the primary
     block's field names, so its `dense_successes` (3,313) holds **P9-B's** successes.
8. **The 15 `bounds` failures** (0.0003 %) are units with no entities recorded. They stay in the
   corpus and remain retrievable by Dense and BM25.

## Next research decision

Phase 9 answers its question: the simple, query-blind, one-hop Entity Hop keeps nearly all of its
complementary value over BGE-small at full HotpotQA FullWiki scale, for 4.57 USD of extraction.

It also records something the historical pool hid. At scale, **BM25 complements Dense slightly
better than the Entity Hop does** at the primary budget (+1.2 pp, p = 0.024, descriptive).
Phase 7 could not see this because the two hybrids were tied there. The two signals are clearly
not the same: 371 questions go the Entity Hop's way and 436 go BM25's.

The candidate directions, none opened here:

- **Dense + BM25 + Entity Hop** (deferred in `research_roadmap.md`). The disagreement counts
  above are the direct motivation. It needs a written spec with weights fitted on dev only; the
  7,405 FullWiki questions are now spent as held-out for Phase 9's systems.
- **Hub control inside the frozen hop** (for example a DF cap). This is a new mechanism and needs
  its own spec. The measured candidate distribution (median 5,504) is the evidence for it.
- **A substantially stronger Dense retriever** remains untested (Phase 8 / 8.1). The FullWiki
  artifacts built here — corpus, BM25, GLiNER index — can be reused by it.

Which of these comes next is the author's decision. No richer graph machinery is opened by this
result.
