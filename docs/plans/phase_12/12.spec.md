## Problem and objective

**Problem.** P10-C (Dense + BM25 + the frozen Phase 9 Entity Hop, 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2) is the line's
best system: 66.50 % Full Support @2,048 on `test-10`, 4,801 / 7,405 on dev. Phase 11 tried two
other uses of the same entities, a DF cap on P1's entities and a hop seeded from the question's
entities. Neither cleared its dev gate (best 4,808 against a bar of 4,838; `DEV_STOP`).

A post-hoc dev diagnostic (`phase_11/11.results.md`, exploratory) looked at the 4,310 gold
paragraphs Dense leaves outside its top 10:

- the P1 hop lists 1,665 of them at depth 100, but at median position 11, and only 590 in its
  first 5;
- 1,290 of the 1,665 tie with other candidates. A candidate's score depends only on which of
  P1's entities it shares, so every paragraph sharing the same entity scores the same;
- the question-seeded hop largely overlaps BM25.

The hop never reads the question. It weighs every entity of P1 by rarity alone, so for "in which
year was the director of *Titanic* born?" it treats James Cameron, Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate
Winslet alike. The resulting hypothesis, which has not been measured, is that the bottleneck is
**choosing which of P1's entities is the bridge**.

**Question.**

> Does choosing, with the question, which of P1's entities the hop starts from (instead of
> starting from all of them) retrieve the full multi-hop evidence more often than P10-C at
> FullWiki scale?

**Objective.** One candidate family, fitted on dev. The single configuration dev selects is
measured once on `test-11`:

- against **P10-C**, which decides the label;
- against **P10-B** (Dense + BM25), reported with its own preregistered test, because it is the
  reference the community understands.

This is the cheap test of roadmap 7.2 ("query awareness at the seed"), run before any relation
(roadmap 8.2) is built.

## Decisions settled before implementation

### D1 — How P1's entities are scored against the question

The GLiNER index stores each paragraph's entity forms but not where they occur. The corpus stores
each paragraph as its list of sentences. So an entity is placed in P1's sentences by text, with
no re-extraction:

- **Location.** An entity node of P1 is *located* in a sentence of P1 when its normalized form
  (normalization v1, `nodes/normalization.py`) occurs, bounded by non-word characters, in the
  normalized sentence text. The title is not part of any sentence. A node located in no sentence
  is *unlocated*.
- **Sentence relevance.** `rel(sentence) = cos(BGE(q), BGE(sentence))`:
  - `BGE(q)` is the question vector already cached for Dense, encoded exactly as Dense encodes
    questions (for BGE-small, with no instruction prefix; corrected on 2026-09-27, see the note
    at the top);
  - `BGE(sentence)` encodes the sentence text **alone, without the title**, as a passage, with
    the Phase 9 Dense model and revision (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, L2-normalized).
- No LLM, no trained component and no extra model is involved.

Rejected alternatives, for the record:

- **Similarity to the entity name:** a bare name carries little meaning, and the entity most
  similar to the question tends to be the one the question already names, not the bridge.
- **Lexical overlap with the sentence:** it misses paraphrase ("director" / "directed by") and
  largely repeats BM25.
- **A window around the mention:** it adds a width parameter to fit.

### D2 — Which seeds are kept

- **Eligible entities.** P1's entity nodes (`config.ENTITY_HOP_TYPES`), minus the excluded ones
  when exclusion is on (below).
- **Sentence ranking.** P1's sentences that hold at least one located eligible entity, ranked by
  `rel` descending, ties by sentence position ascending.
- **Seeds at `s`.** The eligible entities located in the first `s` ranked sentences, with
  `s ∈ {1, 2, all}`. At `s = all` every eligible entity is a seed, unlocated ones included. For
  `s < all`, unlocated entities are never seeds.
- **Exclusion of question-named entities** (`x ∈ {no, yes}`). When `x = yes`, an entity node of
  P1 is not eligible if its normalized form occurs, bounded by non-word characters, in the
  normalized question text. This is a text rule: no GLiNER runs at query time, and the Phase 11
  question-entity artifacts are not read.
- **Scoring.** The seeds enter the unchanged Phase 9 hop: same rarity weights, same `read(q)`
  exclusion, same depth 100 and tie-break by unit id. A question whose seed set is empty yields
  an empty list.
- **Identity.** `s = all, x = no` is exactly the Phase 9 hop, and so P10-C's third component.

A consequence, declared as a limitation: seed selection decides **which group** of candidates
rises. It does not order candidates that share the same seeds; they still tie and break by unit
id.

### D3 — The candidate family and the dev fit

P12 is `weighted` fusion with min-max normalization per component, the scheme P10-C used, over
three components in fixed order: Dense, BM25, the seeded hop at `(s, x)`.

- **Grid:** 6 seed configurations `(s, x)` × the 66 weight triples of multiples of 0.1 that sum
  to 1, that is **396 configurations**.
- **The grid contains P10-C exactly:** `s = all, x = no`, `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2`. It also contains
  an exclusion-only control, `s = all, x = yes`.
- **Objective:** Full Support @2,048 over the 7,405 dev questions.
- **Ties** are broken by:
  1. gold recall @2,048;
  2. `x = no` before `x = yes`;
  3. the larger `s` (`all` is the largest), the one closest to P10-C;
  4. the larger `w_dense`;
  5. the larger `w_bm25`.

### D4 — Laptop reproduction gate

Before the fit, the new code evaluated at the P10-C point (`s = all, x = no`, `0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2`)
must reproduce P10-C's dev count **exactly: 4,801 / 7,405**. It must also leave 0 differing
questions against the Phase 10 dev lists, and give a hop list identical to Phase 10's for every
dev question. A miss stops the phase and goes to the author.

### D5 — Dev gate

The selected configuration must reach **at least 4,835 / 7,405** dev Full Support @2,048, that is
P10-C's 4,801 plus 34 questions.

The margin is Phase 11's 37 questions for 1,430 configurations, rescaled to this grid. The
expected selection gain of the best of K noisy configurations grows as √(ln K), not in proportion
to K:

`37 × √(ln 396 / ln 1,430) = 33.6`, rounded up to 34.

Below the bar, the phase stops with `DEV_STOP` and `test-11` stays unopened.

### D6 — Primary endpoint and label

Full Support @2,048 on `test-11`, P12 against P10-C: one two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial on
the discordant questions, p = 1/2), α = 0.05.

```text
SEED_SELECTION_SUPPORTED      wins > losses and p < 0.05
SEED_SELECTION_REGRESSION     losses > wins and p < 0.05
SEED_SELECTION_NOT_SUPPORTED  otherwise
DEV_STOP                      D5
```

The label is written by code. Nothing else can change it.

### D7 — Secondary comparison, preregistered

P12 against P10-B on the same questions and metric, with the same exact McNemar test. It is
reported with wins, losses, delta and p, and it **cannot change the label**. The report also
gives P10-C against P10-B on `test-11` (descriptive): these are the first `test-11` figures of
either system.

### D8 — Latency, reported, not gated

Each system's `retrieve` wall time per question is measured in the same pass on the laptop, as in
Phase 10:

- it covers Dense scoring, BM25, the hop and fusion, and excludes the BGE question encoding;
- for P12, the encoding of P1's sentences is new query-time work: on `test-11` it happens inside
  the pass, is **timed separately** and is reported beside the `retrieve` figure and summed with
  it;
- the report gives mean, median, p95, max and the ratios P12 / P10-C and P12 / P10-B.

## User stories

### HU-1 — P1's sentence vectors on dev, computed once

**As** the author, **I want** the sentences of every dev P1 encoded once into their own cache,
**so that** the fit reads fixed vectors.

- [ ] P1 is taken from the Phase 10 dev Dense lists (first unit), not recomputed.
- [ ] The vectors go to `data/phase12/cache/sentences/`. Its key covers the model, the revision,
      the text rule (sentence alone, no title) and the set. No existing cache is overwritten.
- [ ] The run records sentence count, seconds and host. The planning figures (about 30,000
      sentences, 10-25 min, about 46 MB) are projections from the Phase 10 and Phase 11 question
      encodes (5,000 questions in 94 s and in 207 s on the laptop).
- [ ] Location coverage is recorded on dev: the share of P1 entity nodes located in some sentence,
      and the share of questions whose seed set is empty at each `(s, x)`.

**As** the author, **I want** proof that the new code reproduces P10-C on dev before anything is
chosen, and the choice made on dev alone, **so that** the held-out comparison is honest.

- [ ] D4 reproduces 4,801 / 7,405 exactly, or the phase stops.
- [ ] The full 396-point curve is recorded, with the selected point and the tie rule applied.
- [ ] The dev gate is applied by code. `DEV_STOP` leaves `test-11` unopened.
- [ ] The selection is committed before the held-out pass.

### HU-3 — One held-out pass

**As** the author, **I want** P10-A, P10-B, P10-C and P12 measured once on `test-11`, **so that**

- [ ] Runs only after the author's explicit authorization, and refuses a second start.
- [ ] Uses the `test-11` question vectors cached in Phase 11 (key `3ab80134c058052d`).
- [ ] Encodes the `test-11` P1 sentences inside the pass, timed per D8, and writes them to their
      own cache file for the record.
- [ ] Keeps per-question outcomes for the paired tests. The budget metrics are recorded at 512,

### HU-4 — Results

**As** the author, **I want** `12.results.md` with the answer against both bars, **so that** the

- [ ] Every value is labelled measured, derived, exploratory or projection.

## Data contracts

- **`data/phase12/cache/sentences/`**: one NPZ per set (dev, `test-11`) with the sentence vectors
  keyed by P1 unit id and sentence position, plus a JSON manifest holding the model, revision,
  text rule, count, seconds and digest.
- **`data/phase12/dev-lists.jsonl.gz`** and its manifest: the seeded-hop list per dev question for
  each of the 6 `(s, x)`, depth 100, with the seeds used. The manifest records the digests of the
  Phase 10 dev lists and the sentence cache it reads.
- **`data/phase12/reproduction.json`**: the P10-C dev count through the new code, against 4,801,
  with the differing qids and the hop-list equality check.
- **`data/phase12/fit.json`**: the grid, the curve, the selected configuration, the tie rule, the
  dev gate verdict and P10-C's dev count. Committed before the pass.
- **`data/phase12/run-<system>.json`** and **`outcomes-<system>.jsonl.gz`**: the Phase 10 run
  contract, with provenance naming `test-11`, its vectors, the sentence cache and each system's
  own weights.
- **`data/phase12/outcome.json`**: D6, D7, the label, `stop_reasons` and latency (D8).

Every artifact is JSON or NPZ. No pickle. As in Phases 9-11, only `fit.json` is versioned in git;
the other artifacts are recorded by digest in `12.results.md`.

## Restrictions

- **R1.** No rented compute and no paid API. Everything runs on the laptop.
- **R2.** Phase 9, 10 and 11 artifacts are read-only. Phase 12 writes only under `data/phase12/`.
- **R3.** Nothing is fitted or selected on `probe`, `test-10` or `test-11`. `test-10` is spent: it
  is neither retrieved on nor read again, not even descriptively.
- **R4.** P10-B and P10-C keep their frozen weights. The hop keeps its scoring, depth, `read(q)`
  exclusion and tie-break by unit id.
- **R5.** Sentence vectors use the Phase 9 Dense model and revision. Any other encoder is a
  different phase.

---

## Anti-objectives

Phase 12 does **not**:

- re-extract the corpus, rebuild the entity index or change the normalization;
- canonicalize entities, link them to titles or articles, use hyperlinks or entity types;
- add relations, a second hop, seeds outside P1, several P1s or graph propagation;
- re-rank or filter hop candidates by their own relevance to the question (the candidate-side
  form of roadmap 7.2), or change the tie-break inside a seed group;
- weight seeds softly, add a temperature or learn anything;
- run GLiNER, an LLM or a cross-encoder at query time;
- refit P10-B or P10-C, or try a second candidate family after a `DEV_STOP`.
